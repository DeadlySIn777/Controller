## Actual channel and transport limits

`gateware/rtl/cnc_endpoint.v:17` accepts two ABZ buses; line 18 exposes six STEP/DIR outputs; lines 160–162 advertise six steps, two encoders and zero frequency channels. `cnc1/src/cnc1.h:8` fixes frames at 64 bytes, and lines 39–60 retain six/two-sized driver arrays and an 8-bit mask. `cnc1/src/hal_cnc1.c:23` has six scale pins and the driver build links `sim_transport.cpp`, not spidev.

The fixed v1/v2 command uses a 16-byte header, six 32-bit rates and CRC at bytes 62–63; ordinary status contains six emitted counters, two encoder counters and two index latches. Twelve rates alone plus the unchanged header and CRC would require 66 bytes, before any additional control/status. Increasing connector count or changing a capability description cannot extend this ABI. Main's older draft v3 makes masks 16-bit and frames 128 bytes and describes twelve/four plus configuration/identity behavior; no main-branch codec/RTL/driver/test implementation of that draft was found. Its scratch probes are excluded from repository implementation evidence. The separate IS-1 branch supersedes that wire proposal with an actual 176/96-byte host codec/core/model, discussed below; it is not the same draft and is not merged.

The main draft v3 wire arithmetic accounts for two transactions per cycle: 410.56 us at 5 MHz or 328.64 us at 6.25 MHz for two 128-byte frames including the specified minimum guards. The separate IS-1 specification uses 176-byte CS_FB plus 96-byte CS_CMD, twelve 24-bit step counts and twelve 24-bit encoder slots, with specified full-I/O status. Its corresponding wire/guard arithmetic is 436.16 us at 5 MHz or 349.12 us at 6.25 MHz. Neither set is a measured ioctl/controller/pad-delay/LinuxCNC/scheduling result or proof of the 700 us completion target.

`cnc1/src/spidev_transport.c` is an actual compiled Linux backend with mocked-syscall tests: 892 normal assertions, 892 ASan/UBSan assertions and C++ linkage. It issues synchronous `SPI_IOC_MESSAGE(1)` and has no enforced completion deadline. Its advisory flock cannot alone prove exclusive physical bus ownership. The HAL build currently uses only Verilator transport. A successful retained SPI mock test is not physical SPI evidence.

The current DDS limit is 195,312.5 steps/s per channel, with default high/low/setup/hold of 100 ticks at 50 MHz (2 us). That is below the later expanded 500 ksteps/s study goal and does not meet every drive's required timing. Actual-runtime scale validation includes a two-count-per-servo correction margin: at the 1 ms profile the admitted rate is 193,312.5 steps/s before dividing by joint velocity. Encoder plausibility uses a logical RTL edge ceiling rather than a qualified cable/receiver limit. Emitted STEP counters are command accounting; they do not establish measured mechanical position or a tuned servo loop.

## Unmerged CNC3 implementation and remaining boundaries

The branch's `cnc3/README.md`, `gateware/v3/README.md` and `cnc3/model/cnc3_model.h` accurately separate host code from hardware. Python reads the IS-1 JSON layouts and derives structural rules; generated C headers and golden vectors are fresh. C implements CRC-16 host frames and CRC-32C IOL1 frames, command building/validation, feedback/status/capability decoding, twelve-channel rate arrays and 24-bit counter unwrap. Its host driver implements discovery, qualification, CONFIGURE, SYNC/ARM/UPDATE/DISARM, first-fault latching, bounded freshness, acknowledgment checks, expansion-mask refusal, motion count plausibility, event storage/acknowledgment and telemetry storage. The host tests exercise all configured channels, counter wrap over 100 simulated seconds, malformed frames, absent/removing expansion, watchdog, stale samples and refusal of a too-long period.

The model is a behavioural test double with closed-form DDS accumulation. It is not pulse-accurate RTL and does not model encoders, probes, frequency inputs, PWM, IOL1 card links, telemetry acquisition or general I/O opcodes other than IO_DISABLE. Its self-test state does not constitute a measured external-watchdog test. There is no v3 HDL, HDL testbench, target synthesis/fit, pin plan, HAL component/pin publication, spidev or Verilator transport, or LinuxCNC profile in this branch. STM32 I/O-card firmware, updates, replacement identity, full configuration-service integration, HR1/HR2 persistent history and commercial entitlement remain specified work.

The frozen IS-1 baseline still caps STEP at 195,312.5 steps/s. Its master specification explicitly labels the expanded 2,000,000 steps/s qualification goal unmet. Twelve counters and all-channel model motion are not a result at that higher rate, a servo tuning result or physical position evidence.

The following are **source-inspection findings to close before integrating this core**; they were not new fault-injection tests and are not assertions about an operating physical machine:

1. `cnc3/src/cnc3.c:43` validates the live configuration's ranges but does not freeze or compare a configured snapshot. The public driver struct exposes mutable configuration; `accept_feedback` uses its current scale at line 120 and `cnc3_write` uses it for target conversion at line 335. The production configuration/HAL integration must refuse live changes or reconfigure through the stopped, validated transaction rather than silently consuming mutable scale/timing/masks.
2. `cnc3/src/cnc3.c:110` bounds emitted-step counter deltas; line 113 unwraps encoder counts without an encoder-rate plausibility bound, and required encoder acquisition/age/validity is not implemented by the model. Codec slots alone do not establish trustworthy physical feedback.
3. `cnc3/src/cnc3.c:240` stores telemetry by `(source * 7 + page) % 64`. It does not expire entries or invalidate an earlier valid reading when a later status is invalid. Production validity/age publication must implement the specified disconnected/stale behavior and define collision/eviction behavior; this mirror is unfinished storage.
4. `cnc3/src/cnc3_proto.c:258` checks response length, CRC, magic and ABI. CNF3/CNS3 decoders then accept fields without enforcing their reserved-zero/high-mask/status-bit rules; CAP3 separately checks its reserved bytes. Add resealed malformed-response cases and explicit host-side semantic validation before claiming complete IS-1 receive validation. CRC detects transmission corruption; it does not make a structurally malformed but correctly resealed response valid.
5. Event storage is bounded host RAM and acknowledged after storage. It is not power-loss-tolerant history. A host fault sends NOP and relies on the endpoint watchdog after feeding stops; physical propagation and the independent timer must be separately qualified.

The four codec interpretations listed in `gateware/v3/README.md` (SELFTEST's overlapping byte rule, inherited channel-1 I/O rules, structural versus semantic CONFIGURE rejection and JSON authority over Markdown) need incorporation into the authoritative specification revision before independently implementing RTL/card firmware. Pinning a chosen interpretation in tests makes it reproducible, not automatically agreed across implementations.

## Evidence strength and limits

The current CNC1 manifest records 220 compiled C/RTL assertions, 27 volatile LinuxCNC checks, 68 persistent checks across nine independent launches, 41 runtime host tests and 33 persistent-policy host tests. The runtime profile maps three LinuxCNC joints; the compiled harness covers all six generators. The persistent service deliberately demonstrates initialization, saved scaling, interruption/recovery and wrong/missing/corrupt/older authority refusal. No saved state restores homing or enables motion. It persists only supported scales; full machine/wiring/tool/profile cartridge authority is separate.

Yosys mapped the six/two RTL to 9,426 Efinix-family primitive cells. Mapping and the 179-assertion frozen post-map harness establish a functional family-mapping result. They do not establish Efinity package fit, T20 pin directions, routed resource utilization, 50 MHz timing closure, target boot/configuration or a usable bitstream. The present assembly's additional FPGA balls are circuit reservations, not active RTL ports.

The ESP manifest retains compiled ESP32-S3 images of 177,376 and 202,352 bytes for default and UART-check configurations. Default receive GPIO is -1; UART-check GPIO4 is explicitly a compile fixture, not a deployed approved image. The firmware reports `motion_authority: false`; observation timing is 1,000 ms link freshness and 500 ms backend progress freshness, evaluated when the ESP parses bytes. Buffered bytes and unauthenticated boot identity make this unsuitable as permission/recovery authority. No target flash or radio/USB/UART behavior was measured.

The file-only configuration service correctly rejects altered capability limits, bad schema/numeric input, resource conflicts, stale revision/epoch plans and armed/running/connected mutation. Its checksums detect corruption, not authentication. The cartridge has two bounded 16,384-byte record slots; physical memory/page-write/power interruption is untested. Ordinary full-profile edits do not update its binding/cartridge automatically; later replacement refuses pending explicit reconciliation. The actual persistent scale service has its own coordinated journal/witness and compiled inhibit, but both witness and journal are still on one filesystem.

## Commercial boundary assessment

The documented policy covers controller sales, paid software, expansions, complete kits, customer-sourceable peripherals, tested compatibility, offline use and replacement without repurchase. A scan of current executable software/schema/tests found no entitlement/licence/activation implementation. New CNC3 files declare `SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-Proprietary`; that is a source label, not executable paid offline entitlement, a complete commercial terms file or approval of the LinuxCNC distribution boundary. No complete release SBOM or commercial original-software licence file was found in the inspected product scope. Captured Debian package-version lists and SDK commit identities are useful provenance, but are not a complete dependency/licence/notice/source-offer inventory.

`cnc1/src/hal_cnc1.c:12` declares GPL and includes LinuxCNC RTAPI/HAL headers, then is linked as a shared LinuxCNC module. The commercial application/service arrangement needs deliberate review of linking, IPC, modified components, copied code/assets and bundled scripts for the actual distribution. This audit makes no legal determination that a process split does or does not satisfy a licence. A paid licence must never override an applicable third-party obligation or block stopping/inhibit, diagnostics, recovery or rollback. Offline entitlement and replacement tests must cover missing/corrupt records, clock problems, compute/motion/I/O replacement, incompatible identity and absent network/AI.
