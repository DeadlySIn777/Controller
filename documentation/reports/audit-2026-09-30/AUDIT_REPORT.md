## Findings that prevent completion

1. **A real reset-buffer pin defect escaped the passing checks.** Main U4 `74LVC1G17GW` is drawn as A=1/GND=2/NC=3. The manufacturer's GW package is NC=1/A=2/GND=3, with Y=4/VCC=5. The builder, custom symbol and validator repeat the same wrong assumption. Its real input would be grounded and real ground disconnected. ERC and mirrored mutation tests cannot discover incorrect manufacturer semantics in their own expected model. This was an error in the earlier generated design and its validation; the audit records it explicitly rather than calling the reset path fixed. [Nexperia datasheet, Table 3](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74LVC1G17.pdf), [H01/H06](HARDWARE_AUDIT.md).
2. **The drawn independent timer fails the retained 20 ms fault-to-inhibit requirement.** Main TPS3431/120 pF is approximately 64.3 ms nominal and about 71.8 ms in its screened upper case. Its internal timing offset prevents reducing it to 20 ms by a smaller capacitor. A clocked 5 ms RTL timer cannot cover a stopped FPGA clock. The permission bench manually injects watchdog failure and tests the digital response, not the analog detection time. IS-1 specifies a different dual-TPS3851 network with bounded arithmetic; it is not drawn or measured. [TI TPS3431 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3431.pdf), [H02](HARDWARE_AUDIT.md).
3. **The current integrated power design does not close the expanded loads.** Required twelve-motor normal/fault/startup cases and an eight-motor startup case exceed the retained 6 A main screen. IO3V3 and ESP combined capacitance also need effective-capacitance/impedance/stability acceptance. AM26C31 partial-power input compliance, clamp coordination and reset retained-charge/JTAG release remain open. These are source/arithmetic findings and unclosed constraints, not measured oscillation or a physical fault trial. [H03–H07](HARDWARE_AUDIT.md), [expanded review](../EXPANDED_POWER_REVIEW.md).
4. **The adopted interfaces and serviceable product are not built.** Eight standard plus plug-in four, individual enables/alarms, 24 general inputs/16 general outputs, four base encoders plus concurrent expansion, validated serialized cards, measured diagnostics, fixed wiring base and replaceable motion card are mandatory recorded requirements. IS-1 develops their specification; the current drawings/runtime do not implement them. General I/O cannot silently be consumed by dedicated motor/home functions. [H08–H11](HARDWARE_AUDIT.md), [22 acceptance gates](../../docs/CURRENT_ACCEPTANCE_GATES.md).
5. **There is a substantial cost mismatch in the new modular proposal.** IS-1 section 02 estimates the quantity-100 eight-motor/four-encoder controller at **US$955–1,500 manufactured cost before the screen**, with proposed selling price **US$1,739–2,729** at the stated 45% margin. These are unquoted engineering estimates with unknowns, not a completed BOM or supplier offer. They greatly exceed the preliminary US$400 controller-only reference. This does not prove every custom architecture is uneconomic; it proves the current specified modular package has not met the commercial objective and needs a full cost/architecture review. The historical US$249/Mesa bundled examples must not be substituted for this scope. [Separate IS-1 cost section](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-f1036d2a3408cdd7).
6. **The main and unmerged implementations are still distinct.** Main has a 64-byte CNC1 six/two path. The older expansion study's 128-byte draft is historical design input. Current IS-1/CNC3 has 176-byte feedback and 96-byte command transactions and an eight/twelve host model. It lacks the matching RTL, HAL, physical spidev/I/O/service integration. Prototype concerns identified in the software audit include configuration mutation/continuity and encoder/telemetry validity limits; passing codec/model checks are not permission to operate a machine. [Software audit](SOFTWARE_AUDIT.md), [branch check receipt](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-cc90cc9ff2131579).
7. **The differentiating customer experience and paid release remain mostly specifications.** Preserve the requested UI, easy model/no-CAD setup, guided wiring, hardware-backed faults, fast offline repair, all screen tiers and legitimate licence continuity. Existing metadata/file/model tools are useful foundations but do not deliver those complete workflows. There is no evidence that this product currently outperforms MASSO, Acorn or Mesa on a defined machine benchmark.
8. **The frozen IS-1 specification also needs independent corrections.** Its 12,858 validator checks pass, but hardware review finds that history records omit emitted/encoder counts and sampled diagnostics promised for the fault snapshot; the selected receiver's shorted/terminated-input fail-safe claim is not guaranteed by its manufacturer; Ioff buffers do not themselves establish 24 V miswire or AM26C31 partial-power compliance; and the selected angled/angled motion-card connector pair needs a mechanical mating-direction drawing or corrected pair. These are specification defects/feasibility gaps, not fabricated-board measurements. Frozen-source consistency does not prove the specification physically works. Preserve IS-1 and make reviewed corrections through its revision process. [Separate-worktree hardware review](HARDWARE_AUDIT.md).

No runtime, gateware, circuit or physical design defect was silently repaired during this audit. The schematic sources, existing machine configurations, frozen IS-1 revision and historical evidence were preserved. The changes made here reconcile requirements, preserve a missing source, add explicit gates and expose findings.
