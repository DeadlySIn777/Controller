# Standalone IS-1 dual-watchdog frontend

30 September 2026. The new editable candidate is [is1_watchdogs.kicad_sch](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-d2391c7dca34efaf), with [design data](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-37fca6f9d4fcbeb5), native [netlist](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-40a935d51f69adbc), [ERC](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-f2cd0ad1017fcb4e), [validation receipt](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-ebefaae742df4865), BOM, symbol library and a DRB copper-pad candidate. It is **not included in the connected assembly** and supplies only timer frontends. Existing hardware, frozen IS-1, CNC3 and historical evidence were not changed. No PDF was exported by this implementer.

The circuit contains two TPS3851G18SDRBT standard-timing supervisors, each with its own exact Samsung CL10C332JB8NNNC 3.3 nF C0G/50 V/±5% timing capacitor, separate SN74LVC1G34DBVR WDI buffers and two channels of one SN74LVC2G17DBVR health buffer. Both RESET and WDO open-drain outputs of each timer share one health node with a 10 kΩ pull-up to IO_3V3. The Schmitt outputs are WD_M_OK and WD_O_OK. Both timers' physical MR pin 3 remains explicitly unconnected, using its internal pull-up as frozen IS-1 requires; there is no FPGA_LOCAL_RESET_N connection or customer MR terminal. GND pin 4 and exposed pad 9 are LOGIC_GND.

SET1 pin 5 receives FPGA_MOTION_REQUEST or FPGA_OUTPUT_REQUEST, with 100 kΩ pulls. Each WDI buffer input/output and each health output also has a 100 kΩ pull-down. Five individual 100 nF capacitors cover the five ICs. There are **22 physical parts** (five ICs, seven capacitors, ten resistors); the separate INTERNAL REVIEW BOUNDARY symbol is excluded from the BOM and board and is not a fitted connector.

Primary retained TI Rev B June 2025 [TPS3851 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3851.pdf), TI [single-buffer datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g34.pdf) and TI [dual Schmitt-buffer datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g17.pdf) supply the physical pin tables. Sources and hashes are preserved under [research/is1-watchdogs](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-ce66a3caae5cdeb2). The validator extracts those primary tables independently of the builder, compares native symbol functions and physical conductors, and checks exact parts, packages, no-connect markers, pulls, decoupling, boundary exclusion and scope flags. TI's DRB0008A drawing supplies the candidate copper dimensions; stencil/paste segmentation, thermal vias, CWD guard layout, cleaning and routing remain unqualified.

The final local checks passed: **31 pin/net/part/scope mutation tests** and **zero native KiCad 10.0.4 ERC violations**. Mutations include a coherently wrong old EN3/ENOUT8 timer map, coherently swapped buffer input/ground, missing MR no-connect, shared timing nodes, crossed progress/health channels, bypassed WDI buffer, RESET missing from health, wrong capacitor/extended-timing variant, missing decoupling, pad-map faults, fitted boundary and overstated startup/manufacturing claims. The receipt records source hashes. These are source/native-circuit consistency results, not electrical measurements or independent-watchdog timing tests.

Important unresolved integration requirements:

- **Startup defaults are unqualified.** The T20's configuration/reset GPIO weak pull-up can defeat a 100 kΩ request/progress pull-down (about 2.73 V at 3.465 V and a 27 kΩ pull-up). Even a parallel historical 10 kΩ pull is not a guaranteed TPS3851 SET1 low. Keep these literal IS-1 pulls but review FPGA pin conditioning and any versioned interface correction before integration. LVCMOS33's specified driven-low limit has only a small SET1-low margin; LVTTL's higher limit is insufficient for that claim. Future CDONE/arm/permission gates are not present here.
- **Health pull-downs do not qualify partial power or brownout.** G18 supply sensing is a roughly 1.8 V-class threshold, not proof that IO_3V3 meets FPGA/buffer operating limits. Low-supply undefined states, driver leakage, ramp/slew and health loading still need a complete rail/permission review and measurements.
- **New progress firmware/logic is required.** SET1 low disables its timer. WDI feeds on falling edges; IS-1 requires a 1 µs high pulse, idle low, per accepted motion ARM/UPDATE and appropriate full-I/O progress. Historical toggle-per-UPDATE logic is not this interface. Timeout RESET/WDO pulses are not sticky permission latches; complete deliberate re-arm/default logic remains future work.
- **Timing is an arithmetic screen.** Root-owned [is1_watchdog_analysis.py](../../WORKSPACE_REFERENCES.md#artifact-57230d91feb796e0) distinguishes IC/capacitor tolerances, leakage, temperature/parasitic assumptions, downstream allocation and physical proof. The TI 150 µs SET1 setup entry is nominal, so a guaranteed self-test window is not closed by that calculation. This block claims neither measured ≤20 ms fault-to-inhibit nor mechanical stopping, redundancy, safety certification or manufacturing readiness.

Rebuild only this candidate with `python tools/hardware_build_is1_watchdogs.py`, followed by native netlist/ERC exports into its own directory. Run `python tools/hardware_validate_is1_watchdogs.py --check` for read-only consistency and `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_is1_watchdogs.py -v` for the narrow mutation suite. Integration requires a reviewed complete permission chain and controlled interface/evidence handoff; the new block must not be automatically copied into the current assembly.
