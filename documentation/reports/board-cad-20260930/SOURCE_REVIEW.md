# Board CAD source inventory and placement-study review

30 September 2026. Read-only inventory before root creates the new placement study. No electrical source, BOM assignment, frozen IS-1 or old evidence was modified by this reviewer. No physical board has been fitted/manufactured here; “physical candidates” below means sourced component entries intended for future assembly.

The live project initially contained **no `.kicad_pcb`, STEP/STP, FreeCAD, STL, GLB/GLTF, OBJ, WRL or other searched 3D board assets** outside `reports/` and `tmp/`. Nine live custom `.kicad_mod` files exist, including assembly-library duplicates; they are footprints, not a board layout. Historical copied snapshots were excluded.

All 444 assigned assembly footprints resolve in the installed KiCad library or the assembly's custom libraries. Unassigned real entries include clamps/fuses, core/field/SBC power parts, bulk capacitors and most ESP helper components; the 126 must not be silently converted into fabricated packages. The six historical receiver/alarm channels and twelve STEP/DIR reservations are not eight/twelve completed motor channels.

Two **placement-only** stock candidates were independently checked without changing schematic/BOM assignments:

- `Package_BGA:TFBGA-256_13x13mm_Layout16x16_P0.8mm`: all **256 unique pad numbers match all actual native T20 symbol balls**, which are sourced from the manufacturer's T20F256 workbook. Corner centers are A1(−6,−6), A16(6,−6), T1(−6,6), T16(6,6) mm; pitch is 0.8 mm; circular copper pads are 0.32 mm. Body/pitch match [Efinix T20 v6.0, Table 3, page 5](https://www.efinixinc.com/docs/trion20-ds-v6.0.pdf): 13 × 13 mm, 0.8 mm. The stock description references the Trion packaging guide v4.9, but that guide was not successfully fetched during this review. Full package/ball orientation, land-pattern/process review and exact height remain qualification gates; a matching pad-name set alone is not footprint approval.
- `Oscillator:Oscillator_SMD_Abracon_ASE-4Pin_3.2x2.5mm`: pads 1–4 and their numbering match the actual Y1 source nets and manufacturer functions: STBY1, GND2, output3, VDD4. [Abracon ASE datasheet, revision 18 February 2022, mechanical page 5](https://abracon.com/Oscillators/ASEseries.pdf) was visually inspected: body 3.2 ±0.2 × 2.5 ±0.2 mm, maximum height 1.2 mm, recommended pads 1.3 × 1.1 mm with center separations 2.10 × 1.65 mm. The stock centers (±1.05, ±0.825 mm) match; pad1 is lower left in the footprint's top view. Y1 source nets are IO_3V3 on pins1/4, LOGIC_GND on2 and FPGA_CLK_SOURCE on3.

The BGA pad-number set was also checked against a fresh independent extraction of the original manufacturer's `t20-pinout.xlsx` T20F256 worksheet: all 256 balls match, and every actually connected U1 pin has a corresponding candidate pad. This is separate from matching the generated native symbol.

Both stock candidates reference STEP files that are **absent** from the installed 3D model directory. Standard resistor/capacitor/SOIC/SC70/SOT23 models used by the already-assigned core/watchdog footprints do exist. A schematic assignment and an available package model are separate facts. Any substitute 3D envelope must be labelled with its geometry source and unverified features, especially FPGA height.

Frozen unmerged IS-1 section02, D02-2 (line29), **proposes** standing 100 × 220 mm cards, mating along the 220 mm edge, up to two P1/P2 positions, guides, a lever and one captive screw. D02-3/section7 (lines30,174–187) specify connector families; the MC boundary reserves two har-bus64/160-contact connectors. No exact XY placements, mounting-hole coordinates, base/cage spacing or approved connector mating geometry are supplied. The earlier both-right-angle HB160 mating/standing-card concern remains unresolved. A 100 × 220 mm MC outline is therefore a prospective envelope, not verified mechanical fit. The proposed separate 60 × 45 mm watchdog study is root's new provisional work area, not an IS-1 dimension.

An honest editable study can place the **68 assigned core footprints plus two explicitly identified placement-only candidates**, preserve actual pads/nets, omit fake J1–J10 connector footprints, and show the **22-part watchdog candidate separately**. Root may additionally show the 444 assigned whole-assembly components as an allocation study, but the 13 electrical sheets are not evidence that they form one finished PCB or the modular IS-1 card/base assembly.

The MC's required circuits still missing from that core-only placement are explicit in frozen section02's MC row (line65): integrated dual watchdogs and reviewed startup conditioning; arm/OPI/sticky-trip latches and final TX/OUT/PROC permission gates; card-bound Ioff buffers including MX4 qualification; PERMIT_OUT self-test gate and DIC/safety-input filters; slot presence/power-good status register; complete compute flash-restore interface; local regulation/supervision and identity/temperature circuitry. The separately drawn watchdog frontends do not implement these automatically. No routing, board DRC, PDN/thermal/creepage closure, FPGA fit, manufacturing outputs or physical validation is earned by the new CAD view.
