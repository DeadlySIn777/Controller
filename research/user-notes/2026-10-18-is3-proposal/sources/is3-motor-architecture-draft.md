Motherboard interface revision IS-3 (DRAFT — pending user authorization)
Proposed 18 October 2026. This draft records the user's design decision and its consequences. It supersedes only the motor-capacity and encoder-port sections of IS-2 (docs/MOTHERBOARD_IS2.md); every other IS-2 boundary (I/O counts except as revised below, permission/health semantics, 20 ms inhibit bound, compute/storage/service, qualification obligations) is retained unchanged. No recorded evidence, schematic, board, ledger or gateware artifact is modified by this draft. The 8-plus-custom-card path from the 28 September study is still not what this proposes — the expansion below is a pluggable accessory interface, not the old rack/card family.

Decisions taken (user, 18 Oct 2026)
#	Decision	Effect
D1	8 motor channels fitted (XM0–XM7), drivers expandable via a qualified expansion interface	Reverses IS-2's "all twelve fitted"; shrinks field terminals and driver circuitry; machine needing >8 axes later depends on the expansion interface being built and qualified
D2	6 ABZ encoder ports fitted (XE0–XE5), 6 reserved logical (ENC6–ENC11), spare receiver channels in the quad budget	Covers today's 2-port demand (spindle + handwheel/MPG) plus ~4 more for scales/rotary; going to 8 was declined to keep board size down — scale-heavy machines are a future revision or the accessory interface
D3	2 isolated RS-485 transceivers fitted, 1 in service + 1 spare	One multidrop bus addresses many devices (Modbus/VFD); the spare covers transceiver failure. CAN boundary retained unchanged
D4	Per-connector-group selectable field supply (jumpered/fused 5/12/24 V per bank), not per-pin selection	Per-pin voltage selection is rejected as a wiring-mistake generator; encoders keep the contracted protected 5 V sensor supply per port
D5	Retain three protected DC entries + eFuse branch protection; field-voltage banks individually fused	Matches the existing power-protection strategy; a bank fuse trip must not restart outputs
D6	Output limits retained: TPS274C65ASRHAR at 0.5 A/channel continuous, 2 A per four-output group, with per-channel current/fault/temperature/supply diagnostics	Higher per-channel current is a future option via separately eFused outputs — it is not claimed here
D7	24 general DI / 24 smart DO fitted	Raised from IS-2's 16 DO: the user's live Mesa requirement is ~20 outputs (lights, relays, pneumatics); 24 DO = exactly 6 quad smart-driver ICs, symmetric with 24 DI, 4 spare channels
D8	Analog block: 2 isolated 0–10 V AO unchanged; 8 AI switchable-range (0–10 V / 4–20 mA / 0.5–4.5 V ratiometric, jumpered per channel group), one multiplexed ADC, fused 5 V/24 V excitation per bank	Covers pressure transducers of unknown/mixed signal type without expansion boards; rides on D4/D5 per-bank fused field supply; ~10–15 cm² added area vs ~60–80 cm² removed by the 12→8 motor cut
D9	One controller serves two machines (MR1 and the EMCO 120P retrofit) via machine-identity-bound profiles	I/O is sized for the union of both machines' needs: the MR1 consumes the 8 fitted channels plus the 4-channel expansion (which is therefore a required deliverable, not optional); the EMCO fits well inside the base channels. Each machine keeps its own profile, commissioning record and machine-bound configuration authority — one board, two machines, no shared live machine state
Expansion interface requirements (XM9–XM12, candidate)
The expansion interface is a required qualified deliverable of D1/D9, not an assumed socket. Candidate scope, all subject to E-gate closure:

Signals per channel: the same ten-contact order as the tentative motor map — STEP+, STEP−, DIR+, DIR−, ENABLE_SW1, ENABLE_SW2, ALARM_IN, ALARM_COM, SIGNAL_0V, SHIELD/CHASSIS. Four channels per expansion connector → 8 + 4 = 12 total channels when fitted.
Physical: keyed, coded pluggable connector family with the same current/touch/shield requirements as the base motor terminals; no backplane, no custom daughterboard ecosystem.
Identity/presence: real, independently checked presence/identity evidence only — the IS-2 rule against forged card identities (mx4 installed, slot class, fake hw.modules entries) applies verbatim. An unqualified or absent expansion state is reported unavailable/stale, never as a believable zero.
Permission/health propagation: expansion channels inherit every output permission condition — the 20 ms fault-to-inhibit bound, arm latches, watchdog coverage and fail-closed defaults cover expansion outputs as if fitted. An open, stale or misconfigured expansion link must inhibit, not drift.
Versioned capability: HAL/service capability negotiation declares fitted base channels and any qualified expansion channels explicitly; no silent ABI success with invented identities. The 96/176-byte transaction discipline is unchanged.
Electrical envelope: same STEP/DIR differential envelope, enable-switch isolation and alarm-input protection requirements as the base channels; expansion supply/return and shield/chassis terminations are separately fused and terminated.
Encoder ports
XE0–XE5 fitted (6 ports): differential A/B/index per port, protected 5 V sensor supply/return/shield per port, same receiver family and open-wire/termination requirements as IS-2.
ENC6–ENC11 reserved logical (6): growth via the qualified optional accessory interface only; no connector or transport selected in this revision. With spindle + handwheel/MPG fitted, 4 ports remain for glass scales/rotary table; a scale-heavy machine needs a future revision or the accessory interface.
6 ports = 48 field contacts (8 per port), vs IS-2's 4-port figure — recompute the terminal map below. Receiver budget: 18 ABZ + 2 frequency = 20 channels; six quad receivers give 24, leaving 4 spare differential receiver channels as circuit spares.
Consequences (explicit, not hidden)
hardware/motherboard/requirements.json and terminal-function-map.csv change: motor positions drop from 12 channels (12 × 14 positions) to 8; encoder positions rise from 4 ports to 6. The 451-position IS-1 map and the 371/80 split are void for the motor/encoder/DO/AI groups and must be regenerated — exact counts come from recomputing against the machine-readable contract (see is3/is3-delta-summary.json in this repo): fitted 359 + reserved 60 = 419 total, −32 vs IS-2.
Gateware/SIM2 impact: the current canonical core maps 12 generators / 4 encoders at 15,805 LUT4 + 2,107 ADD = 17,912 (63 RAM5K). Reducing to 8 generators shrinks that; adding expansion-channel handling adds a small amount back. A remap of the revised architecture is required before any board-resource claim.
Cost audit v11 basis is void: the $1,091.51/$877.37/$796.51 figures were computed for the all-twelve fitted board. A new named BOM for the 8-fitted architecture at 1/10/100 units is required; nothing transfers automatically.
Driver sourcing: 8 channels need 2 quad transmitters (AM26C31-class) instead of 3; encoder receivers rise from 2 quad devices toward 6 (18 ABZ + 2 frequency channels + spares). Enable/alarm circuitry drops by 4 channels. The analog block changes from 4 AI to 8 switchable-range inputs (one multiplexed ADC, fused per-bank excitation); the two isolated 0–10 V AO paths are unchanged. All subject to the E3 worst-case envelope review.
What this draft does NOT do
Does not authorize board layout, schematic edits, gateware changes or any purchase. The native KiCad board, revision ledger and live machine are untouched.
Does not establish physical, electrical, timing or manufacturing qualification of anything.
I/O counts are revised by D7/D8 (24 DI / 24 DO, 8 AI, 6 ABZ); permission/health semantics, 20 ms inhibit bound, and compute/service/storage boundaries are retained unchanged.
