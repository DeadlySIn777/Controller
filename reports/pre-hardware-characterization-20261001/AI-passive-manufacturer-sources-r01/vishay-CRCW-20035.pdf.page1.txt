APPLICATION INFORMATION
When the resistor dissipates power, a te mperature rise above the ambient tempera ture occurs, depend ent on the thermal  
resistance of the assembled resistor together with the printed circuit board. The rated dissipation applies only if the permitted 
film temperature is not exceeded. 
These resistors do not feature a limited lifetime when operated  within the permissibl e limits. However, re sistance value drift 
increasing over operating time may result in exceeding a limit acceptable to the specific application, thereby establishing a 
functional lifetime.
