# Replaceable electronics and machine memory

28 September 2026, updated for the user's 30 September 2026 one-motherboard decision. **Current service requirement:** approved purchased compute, ESP and storage/settings modules are power-off replaceable; the FPGA, motor, field-I/O, analog/comms and power/protection circuits must be integrated on one custom active motherboard. A failed soldered circuit is serviced by exchanging that motherboard through keyed pluggable terminal/harness groups, without reterminating individual conductors or rebuilding saved machine setup. This explicitly supersedes the fixed passive base/removable custom-card service architecture. Whole-board exchange moves terminal groups and changes more circuitry/calibration dependencies than a commodity-module exchange; its time, access, spare cost and affected checks need separate measurement, with no inherited five-minute card-swap promise. Legitimate same-capability board/module replacement preserves paid offline entitlement without repurchase. The document records required service/recovery behavior, not a completed storage, programming, licensing or qualified board. Preserve the unchanged [original note](../research/user-notes/2026-09-28-repairability-memory.txt); the versioned physical transition is [IS-2](MOTHERBOARD_IS2.md), with [project memory](../PROJECT_MEMORY.md) and [commercial policy](COMMERCIAL_MODEL_AND_LICENSING.md).

Keep historical six-step/two-encoder and eight-plus-four-card results distinct from the current all-twelve-interface motherboard/four-ABZ target. Individual enable/alarm on all twelve motors, 24 general DI/16 smart DO, actual electrical limits and independent 20 ms fault-to-inhibit remain required. A plan or fitted Ioff buffer does not establish runtime capacity, complete field interfaces or manufacture readiness. All [screen sizes](DISPLAY_TIERS.md) use the same recovery principles and full I/O; US$400 is a preliminary assembly reference with complete packages separately costed.

## 1. Retain conductor terminations through service

Use one active custom motherboard with keyed pluggable terminal/harness groups. Individual conductors remain terminated in their labeled plugs; whole-board service disconnects and moves these retained groups. Validate access, retention, orientation, shielding and the potential for a wrong-group connection. The former passive base/custom-card rack is preserved history, not a second mandatory PCB or current service boundary.

| Boundary | Current service direction | Feasibility and proof gate |
|---|---|---|
| Linux compute | Qualified purchased CM4/CM5 or separately verified BTT CB2/CB1 module | Exact module/revision, common safe pin/power/reset wiring, thermal/boot image/driver/profile and loaded runtime; connector shape is insufficient |
| ESP/service | Qualified purchased replaceable ESP32 board/module | Socket/mechanics/antenna, protected power/reset/service ownership, compatible programmed image and essential-service loss/recovery |
| Storage/settings | Qualified removable commodity media with actual protected recovery data, local mirror and offline export | Exact medium/controller, capacity/endurance/interrupted-save, write protection/fault containment, machine identity and deliberate authority rules; no custom cartridge PCB is implied |
| Motherboard | Whole-board replacement for soldered FPGA/motor/encoder/field-I/O/analog/comms/power circuits | Retained terminal/harness groups, exact board revision/serial and integrated-region capabilities/calibration, preprogrammed/tested spare, broad affected-function verification, actual service time and spare cost |
| Optional process/accessory interfaces | Separate rated purchased or deliberately justified specialty interfaces | Plasma/THC, encoder/analog, pneumatic, fieldbus and other added capabilities have explicit electrical/timing/identity/calibration contracts; ordinary supported serial devices remain customer-sourceable |

Key groups by electrical function and voltage domain, not color alone. A power plug must not fit a signal group or another incompatible group. Include terminal identifiers, pin/orientation drawings, retention, wire-gauge/torque, chassis/shield connection and service access. Whole-board service must verify all moved groups; a preserved plug does not prove correct seating or wiring continuity.

Integrated regions are not removable cards. Describe them through the observed motherboard identity/revision, fitted rated capability map, factory calibration/provenance and monitored rail/fault state. Do not synthesize card-presence pins or fictitious card serials to satisfy old IS-1 fields. A versioned IS-2 hardware/gateware/driver/service transition must define those meanings and fail inhibited on incompatible identities. Actual purchased module/accessory identities remain separate.

Preserve all 24 general DI and 16 smart DO. Any serially communicated I/O state is validated against the expected board/region or real accessory identity, sequence/freshness and CRC before a changed output is applied; missing, stale, corrupt or mismatched traffic cannot replay an old output. Direct protective permission acts independently. Marker-only framing and a watchdog do not replace integrity validation. Local integration does not itself prove bus timing, fault containment or outputs/defaults.

## 2. Separate settings, calibration and executable software

The term **cartridge** in the retained recovery contract below means the qualified removable commodity settings medium/module and its complete machine-bound records. It does not require another custom PCB, a rack slot, or the former SCART mechanical implementation. Exact media, socket/protection, driver and capacity remain engineering choices. The EEPROM/FRAM comparisons and existing virtual-card demonstrations retain their original scope and are not selected physical parts for the current board.

| Information | Primary home | Replacement rule |
|---|---|---|
| Complete supported machine configuration | Machine cartridge, committed local mirror and exportable backup | Preserve machine identity, axis/channel assignments, scales, travel/velocity/acceleration limits, homing, polarity/timing, resource ownership, device/process profiles, approved sequences, automation dependencies and schema/software requirements |
| Board/region or real module identity and factory calibration | Replacement board's or real module's own factory record | Read its class, serial/revision, rated capabilities and its own calibration/provenance; never copy the failed module's calibration onto different electronics |
| Complete-chain verification | Machine configuration/evidence, bound to all relevant module/harness/source identities | A changed module can invalidate the measurement chain even when its factory calibration is valid |
| Firmware, gateware and Linux image | Motherboard and real module programmed storage plus compatible local recovery packages | Replacement ships programmed; version negotiation and a verified offline package address a needed update |
| CAD models, toolpaths, large job files and optional recovery-image archives | Main storage and external backup; larger cartridge storage only if explicitly included | State what the package protects. A settings-only cartridge must not be sold as recovering an entire failed computer's files |
| Live permissions and position confidence | Runtime only | Never restore arm/run commands, active jog, torque permission, homed state or live workpiece registration merely because saved data is valid |

The cartridge must carry the data needed to reconstruct its advertised machine setup, not just a pathname, cloud link, checksum or ID pointing to a failed computer. A dependency manifest names required compatible software and profiles; the supported replacement image supplies those packages offline. For user-authored recipes/macros or custom profiles required by the setup, include their actual bounded contents or declare a larger storage variant. A pointer to missing user content does not meet the recovery promise.

The machine-bound licence record is separate from module identity and factory calibration. Store enough signed/offline-verifiable entitlement information in the protected machine recovery package or an equivalent local authority that a supported replacement can restore the already purchased software after compatibility checks. Do not bind the entitlement only to the failed compute, ESP or motherboard serial. A new expansion capability may require an additional licence, but a same-capability approved spare does not.

Imported macros are data awaiting validation and deliberate execution. Configuration restore does not run them. Electrical maxima come from qualified installed hardware and supported profiles; imported limits can reduce these maxima but cannot increase them. The common [configuration service](CONFIGURATION_AND_CLI.md) supplies validation, structured preview/diff, apply and export to both CLI and future UI.

Factory records need provenance, calibration method/range/units and dependency identity. CRC or a content hash detects accidental corruption; it does not authenticate a supplier, establish measurement accuracy, or prove correct wiring. Define a signed manufacturer descriptor/calibration policy, key provisioning and separately labeled developer/third-party qualification route before production. Openness of the design does not make an arbitrary self-declared capability a measured electrical rating.

## 3. Select storage from the actual recovery payload

No storage IC or capacity is selected. Current compact canonical UTF-8 serialization using `controller_config.service.canonical(controller_config.model.profile(...))` measures **6,680 bytes for `sim-mill-3axis` and 4,562 bytes for `sim-lathe-2axis`**. These are existing virtual profiles, not full future machine recovery packages. Two copies of the larger profile alone occupy 13,360 bytes before any transaction/header/descriptor overhead.

The Microchip **24LC256 is 32,768 bytes**, not 256 kilobytes. It has 64-byte write pages, whole-array hardware write protection and a maximum 5 ms byte/page write cycle. Writes crossing a page boundary wrap within that page, so the driver must split them. Endurance is per page; its table specifies 1,000,000 cycles at +25°C, 5.5 V in page mode. WP is sampled at the write Stop condition, and a protected write can still acknowledge. Readback is therefore essential. These device facts do not prove our power-loss behavior. [Microchip DS20001203Y, pages 1, 5 and 11](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MPD/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/24AA256-24LC256-24FC256-256K-I2C-Serial-EEPROM-DS20001203.pdf)

For scale, the Infineon FM24V02A is also 32 KiB. Its specified endurance is 10^14 read/write cycles with writes performed at bus speed. Retention is temperature-dependent: 10 years at 85°C, 38 at 75°C, or 151 at 65°C. FRAM can suit frequent counters, but it does not make a multi-byte application record atomic or eliminate a second valid copy. [FM24V02A datasheet, pages 1 and 12](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/10/49/infineon-fm24v02a-256-kbit-32k-8-serial-i2c-f-ram-datasheet-en.pdf)

Use the following capacity gate before selecting EEPROM, FRAM or flash:

```text
usable bytes >= 2 × aligned(maximum supported recovery payload + record envelope)
                + independently reserved identity/format metadata
                + required migration/recovery workspace
```

The envelope includes machine/cartridge lineage, format/schema, generation, previous-record hash, exact lengths, compatibility manifest, content integrity and commit metadata. Account for alignment, erase/program units, signatures if required and bounded retained evidence. Measure the **complete** serialized envelope from the implementation, with the largest supported profiles, recipes and custom definitions, then reserve explicit growth space. Test a document just below and just above the bound. Reject an oversized update before invalidating a slot; never silently drop limits, macros or other fields to fit.

The current service accepts documents up to 2,000,000 bytes and keeps bounded history/audit records on host storage. That host-file limit is not a cartridge capacity promise. A 32 KiB device cannot inherit it. Either define a complete, smaller supported recovery schema or provide larger protected flash storage. Compression, if used, needs bounded decompression and a worst-case capacity check; it is not a guaranteed saving. Large history/job/image archives require separately sized storage and backup policy.

The [file-backed replacement demo](../reports/MODULE_REPLACEMENT_DEMO.md) now measures 6,906 bytes per default mill record, or 13,812 bytes for two copies in its modeled 32 KiB budget. That smaller envelope does not yet implement the complete production metadata, predecessor-chain/commit-page protocol or future macro/profile package above. It demonstrates one bounded virtual payload fitting; it does not select a memory part.

Save settings on deliberate successful transactions; do not rewrite an EEPROM page for every motion cycle, telemetry update or boot. Calculate worst-case writes to the hottest metadata/commit page, including retries, migrations and service use, across temperature and product life. Put high-rate counters/logging in RAM, bounded main-storage logging or a justified FRAM area. Larger serial flash needs an explicit erase/wear/brownout design. None of these parts substitutes for an external backup.

## 4. Two committed generations and one authority

This is the required transaction behavior; the disconnected demo is evidence only for the portion its report exercises. Hardware byte/page behavior remains unimplemented and unmeasured.

1. There is one configuration writer through the common service. Acquire its lock, validate the complete candidate against observed capabilities, and bind the preview to the current configuration generation/hash and stopped/disarmed runtime epoch. Recheck these just before commitment.
2. Keep two independently checkable cartridge record slots. Select the inactive/older slot without touching the newest valid committed record. Invalidate and verify that target slot's old commit metadata before replacing its contents. Respect actual write/erase units; the two slots must not share an erase unit or a page affected by either record's commit update.
3. Write the complete payload and envelope, with bounded lengths and checksums. Poll completion as required, then read and validate the whole prepared record. Only after this succeeds, write its separate commit metadata and read it back. A commit marker alone is insufficient: it must bind the exact record length, generation and content hash. Detect torn commit writes; do not assume a byte or page write is atomic under brownout.
4. The newly verified cartridge commit is the persistent authority. Update and verify the main-storage mirror with that same machine/cartridge lineage, generation and hash. Report distinct states such as committed-with-mirror-pending if the mirror fails; do not acknowledge that all copies are synchronized. On uncertainty after a write, reread before retrying and never manufacture a second branch of history.
5. On restart, inspect both slots without writing either. Reject malformed lengths, bad integrity, uncommitted records, unsupported schemas and foreign machine lineage. Select the newest coherent valid committed generation; never use filesystem modification time as authority. Keep the older valid generation as fallback and display when corruption caused recovery from it.
6. If the main mirror trails a coherent cartridge, repair the mirror only from that validated authority under the recovery workflow. If the cartridge is absent/corrupt, or the mirror is ahead, has a different lineage, or conflicts at the same generation, stay disarmed and present an explicit recovery diff. Do not silently choose the highest number or overwrite either copy. Equal generation with unequal hashes and divergent predecessor chains are conflicts, not normal replication delay.

Normal edits require the cartridge, preventing independent host-only histories. Replacing a cartridge from a mirror/export is an explicit recovery transaction with source identity, preview, affected-check invalidation and audit; it is not an automatic new-machine clone. A blank replacement compute image has no configuration authority over the machine cartridge. A cartridge copied from another machine must not silently become the active setup. Define generation exhaustion/migration rather than permitting wrap to zero.

Two records in one chip provide interrupted-update recovery, not protection from chip destruction, electrical overstress or a common bus fault. Preserve a host mirror and user-exportable backup. File replacement/fsync tests cannot establish EEPROM behavior; later fault injection must interrupt every relevant write/commit/mirror stage and prove at least one previously committed valid generation survives under the defined storage fault model.

## 5. Protected power, buses and outputs

Place removable commodity settings media on the protected logic side, away from field voltages and exposed cable domains. Use a keyed, retained qualified socket/module arrangement with identification and clear power-off service instructions; a custom cartridge PCB is not required. Do not socket a bare memory IC as the ordinary customer procedure. Select ESD protection, current limiting, reverse/misconnection protection, decoupling, reset/brownout behavior and power-off leakage/back-power prevention for the actual connection.

Keep its memory bus outside the cyclic motion path. A shorted settings medium or actual accessory/module must not indefinitely hold the compute module's only essential bus or collapse every logic rail. Specify a dedicated bus or isolatable branch, bounded host timeouts, branch power control and fault reporting; test stuck SDA/SCL, rail shorts and partially powered modules. Electrical isolation here means defined fault containment; galvanic isolation is selected only where the electrical-domain analysis requires it.

WP must default to the protected state in hardware during reset, boot, update and fault, with a deliberately bounded write window. Prove partial-power behavior and WP voltage/timing against the selected memory. Default pulls alone do not protect against a faulty writer; the transaction and permission design remain necessary. Do not promise recovery from every conceivable overvoltage.

Outputs remain governed by the reviewed hardware inhibit, reset/default circuit and independent machine protection while compute, memory or service software is absent. A memory fault detected during operation follows the machine's defined stop/hold/after-run policy; it must not indiscriminately remove gravity support, clamping or necessary cooling. It blocks new configuration and subsequent normal-run permission until resolved. The [ESP accessory contract](ESP_ACCESSORY_AUTOMATION.md) retains one owner per output, measured readiness and essential-service dependencies. Repairability does not grant the ESP torch, toolchanger or motion takeover authority.

## 6. Supported replacement workflow

**Stop → save if the controller remains operable → power down the relevant equipment → replace the approved purchased module, or exchange the motherboard through retained labeled terminal/harness groups → power up disarmed → identify and compare → restore compatible assignments → verify the affected functions and all moved groups → deliberately authorize normal operation.** When the failed section prevents saving, recover and display the last valid committed revision; unsaved changes may be lost. Power-off service is the first supported mode. Presence detection does not qualify hot swap.

Compatibility must be predefined using class, exact hardware revision, connector/key and electrical domain, channel capabilities, firmware/gateware protocol versions, host driver and profile requirements, factory calibration provenance and power budget. An unknown or weaker module stays in a diagnostic workflow. Firmware migration must not reinterpret physical units or increase a limit silently.

| Changed section | Preserve when dependencies match | Verify or invalidate |
|---|---|---|
| Whole motherboard | Compatible machine geometry, stored assignments/scales/limits and validated recovery data | New board/region identity and factory calibration, every moved plug, all twelve motor STEP/DIR/enable/alarm interfaces, encoder direction/capture, full DI/DO/process/analog/comms, rails/defaults/watchdog/inhibit/transport and dependent commissioning; power loss invalidates live position |
| Measurement/process module | Unchanged mechanics and compatible source recipe library | Replacement's own factory calibration, full measurement chain, range/polarity/freshness and affected process qualification |
| Compute or main storage | Cartridge's compatible complete machine setup | Supported boot image, software/schema and all required packages, runtime transport, touchscreen/control behavior; no saved run state |
| Purchased ESP/service module | Compatible saved automation requests and thresholds | Real identity/image, protected rail/reset, output ownership/defaults, sensor freshness/readiness and dependent stop/after-run behavior; on-board power repair is whole-board service |

Each result must distinguish observed identity, selected profile, preserved settings and verified behavior. An approved commodity-module exchange should preserve unaffected calibration when the dependency check allows it; motherboard replacement changes integrated electronics and requires its own broader verification. Every power cycle still requires the machine's position/homing recovery. Restored assignments remain inhibited until their affected checks pass. Guided tests that intentionally energize an output or move an axis require a separate scoped commissioning action; restore, discovery and clicking a test list do not issue such commands.

The [disconnected replacement demonstration](../reports/MODULE_REPLACEMENT_DEMO.md) uses virtual identities and the common service. Its virtual grouped home-input-bank example is a historical card-model fixture, not an implemented removable bank in the current motherboard, a physical output module or measured factory calibration. Hardware discovery, automatic import into a replacement computer, factory programming and live affected-function tests remain separate work.

In the current file-only prototype (`controller_config/recovery.py`), an accepted input-bank replacement leaves the configuration unchanged. It is recorded as one same-revision `replace_module` entry in the shared configuration store's audit. The entry's actor names the machine, the cartridge generation, the slot and the old->new module serial; before and after configuration hashes are equal. The entry is written under the store lock after the store, runtime epoch and inventory are rechecked, and before the binding sidecar is rewritten as `applied`. These are two separate atomic file replacements, not one atomic two-file transaction: an interruption between them can leave the audit entry without the updated binding. This is source-inspected and host-tested file behaviour, not cartridge or EEPROM evidence.

## 7. Preprogrammed spares and offline recovery

Ship supported replacement motherboards and programmable purchased modules with compatible images and their own identity/calibration descriptors already programmed and tested. Integrated field-I/O regions belong to the board's factory record, not a claimed passive replacement card. Supply a compatible boot image on a replacement compute module's specified storage. The ordinary replacement experience should need no IDE, external programmer, account, subscription or firmware search.

Provide a versioned local package when an update is necessary, including image integrity/authenticity, hardware compatibility, migration rules and a known recovery route. Updates happen stopped/disarmed with output permissions withheld and sufficient validated power/storage. Preserve the previous supported image and configuration until the new combination passes its defined checks. Schema rollback and firmware rollback must be compatible; a successfully booted old image cannot safely consume an irreversible new-format configuration by assumption.

For the project's pinned ESP-IDF 5.5.4, application OTA requires at least two OTA slots and `otadata`. Enable `CONFIG_BOOTLOADER_APP_ROLLBACK_ENABLE` in the deployed build and confirm a new application only after its first-boot checks, using `esp_ota_mark_app_valid_cancel_rollback()`. Preserve a bootable permitted previous image; security-version restrictions can prevent rollback. Application rollback does not automatically protect configuration migrations, bootloader or partition-table updates. These mechanisms require configuration and fault testing; current diagnostic firmware is not evidence of the complete workflow. [Espressif v5.5.4 OTA documentation](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/v5.5.4/esp32s3/api-reference/system/ota.html)

## 8. Economics, manufacture and proof before offering it

Add explicit cost rows at quantities 1, 10, 100 and 1,000 for the complete motherboard, retained terminal plugs/headers/keys/harnesses, actual commodity compute/ESP/settings modules, identity/calibration and protected storage/bus/write circuitry, mechanical retention, factory programming/serialization/calibration, fixtures/test time, packaging, spare-board/module inventory, warranty repair and software support. No fixed-base/rack/card-cage or custom MX4/XTB costs belong to the adopted base. Show each useful optional accessory's cost/capability difference and actual spare price. Historical card and 144-case compute/display estimates remain comparison evidence, not a complete current motherboard/service quote; unknown blocks remain unknown.

Before the PCBWay package, define exact compatible substitutes, device lifecycle/availability checks, programming files and tool versions, unique-identity allocation, empty-machine/default cartridge state, image hashes, factory calibration method and acceptance limits, whole-unit test sequence, traceability and locked-versus-serviceable storage regions. Require proof of WP and recovery behavior on the programmed assembly. Factory programming and PCBWay test labor are quotation items, not included by inference. Do not order or submit an incomplete fabrication package.

Publish service drawings, compatibility/version tables, offline recovery tools, revisioned replacements and spare-part pricing. Set a support and stocking policy backed by actual supplier information and economics; no lifetime supply guarantee is made here. The proposed product advantage depends on affordable obtainable spares, not merely sockets.

Acceptance proceeds from the disconnected service demonstration to serialized capacity/endurance analysis; schematic/connector review; storage driver and interrupted-transaction models; then actual bus/power interruption, wrong-module, calibration, output-default and guided-service measurements on bounded engineering prototypes. Finally demonstrate each supported purchased-module exchange and the distinct grouped-terminal whole-motherboard service offline, with no individual conductor retermination or ordinary customer programming; record actual time and verification instead of inheriting a five-minute card target. Record source inspection, host tests, target compilation, HDL simulation, FPGA synthesis, ERC/DRC and physical measurements separately in [engineering status](../reports/ENGINEERING_STATUS.md).
