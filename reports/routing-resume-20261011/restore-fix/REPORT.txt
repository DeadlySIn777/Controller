Routing restore audit and isolated correction candidate — 11 October 2026

Saved r07 evidence shows 29 completed pad joins and 17 lost pad joins while total
uncapped unrouted items decreased from 1376 to 1364. All 17 lost nets were covered
by the original restore pass and explicitly refused because another net's new
pass-(c) copper occupied their former route. The original tool continued after a
refusal and saved the board with that old copper absent. The aggregate-count
integration gate accepted the overall decrease. Failure-only candidate omission
does not explain those 17 losses.

r07-attribution.json freezes the per-net trace. inputs/r07-restore-r1.json and
inputs/r07-partition_diff.json preserve the saved reports; source-manifest.json
records their source paths and hashes. The original source snapshot and shared
tool both hashed 330f250f2b4763fe62cf91d87f91fdfb0ce68e77407b23932d0eb578a7e2356f
at completion of the host receipt.

ripup_restore.py is a fixed COPY for review. The shared Z:/ tool was not edited
or installed. It preflights every net with recorded removed copper and every
ripped-net declaration, including nets with no jobs or failures. A declaration
without geometry is rejected. If any candidate cannot restore, the entire step
returns 3 before adding copper or SaveBoard; the report says saved=false. Invalid
input/accounting returns 2. A caller must honor these nonzero exits and retain its
previous accepted board; this candidate does not recover the already saved r07.

Router accounting requires one failure or at least one indexed, nonzero main
power polyline for each unique input job. Unknown, duplicate, conflicting or
omitted outcomes fail closed. Indexed auxiliary vias/stubs/padstubs/necks must
belong to a job with a main route; unindexed auxiliaries require a successful
main route on their net. This is an accounting check, not proof of pad joins.
Only the current signal producer's kind=power jobs are supported. Pair jobs are
rejected rather than inferred as complete. Older router outputs without indexed
main-route records must not be silently reused.

The source's 0.20 mm same-domain, 0.65 mm cross-domain and 0.25 mm same-net hole
checks remain. Duplicate via suppression is stricter: a replacement must match
position, diameter and drill in native units and a matching through via must
exist on the loaded board. Near-colocated non-equivalent or absent vias refuse.
The original track/via geometry, net and lock restoration remains; restored
objects receive new UUIDs as in the original implementation. SaveBoard still
uses aSkipSettings=True. Destination aliases that could overwrite JSON inputs
or board copper with a report/report.part are rejected.

Host verification: 26 test methods and 39 subtest executions pass, no skips.
host-tests.log and host-receipt.json contain the final run and exact hashes.
Tests cover no-job/no-failure restoration, all-or-nothing refusal before mutation,
clearance/hole preservation, geometry and lock restoration, project settings,
failure accounting, duplicate-via equivalence/presence and destination aliases.

The frozen r07 JSON has 416 jobs, 316 failures and 100 indexed main-route jobs.
Its host geometry replay covers all 130 removed/ripped nets and refuses before
Add/Save (return 3). Every historical lost net is refused. The replay reports 53
refused candidates because it conservatively considers all removed nets and the
mock board has no actual vias. This is deliberately stricter than the original
59 failure-only candidates/25 refusals; it does not demonstrate retention of
r07's 29 gains. host-r07-refusal.json is labelled host/mock evidence. Sentinel
input, output and project bytes remain unchanged in these refusal controls.

No native KiCad board was loaded, no DRC/refill or connectivity job was launched,
no heavy slot was used, and no shared tool or board was written. The saved native
r07 reports are historical evidence; the new checks are host/mock evidence only.
The candidate remains unadopted and has no new native qualification.

Before adopting any future routing candidate, refill its zones, run complete
native DRC, measure fresh pad partitions, and require a final before/after
partition diff with lost=0 after all cleanup. The restore preflight cannot replace
that final guard: job outcomes and aggregate unrouted reductions do not prove
that every previously connected pad pair stayed connected. Selective rollback
of conflicting new routes, native recovery of the old losses, and restoring
round 2 to the queue are separate work and were not performed in this audit.
