CSD19538Q3A 100V N-Channel NexFET™ Power MOSFET
1 Features
• Ultra-low Qg and Qgd
• Low-thermal resistance
• Avalanche rated
• Lead free
• RoHS compliant
• Halogen free
• SON 3.3mm × 3.3mm plastic package
2 Applications
• Power over Ethernet (PoE)
• Power sourcing equipment (PSE)
• Motor control
3 Description
This 100V, 49m Ω, SON 3.3mm × 3.3mm NexFET™ 
power MOSFET is designed to minimize conduction 
losses and reduce board footprint in PoE applications.
