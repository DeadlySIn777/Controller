# Actual isolated analog-input source

This source adds four differential voltage/current inputs on one isolated AI group. It contains 166 real fitted parts, 544 electrical pad identities and 549 native pad shapes. Native KiCad ERC has zero errors and warnings without ignored checks. The independent validator compares every native electrical pin to the reviewed model; 101 vendor-land geometry checks and 40 focused mutation tests passed. The exact receipt is `reports/motherboard-analog-input-20260930/receipt.json`. These results do not qualify a routed or assembled board.

Each input uses INA149 with both reference pins on the same low-impedance 2.5 V Kelvin node. The transfer is differential input +2.5 V, followed by a precision half divider and an actual OPA4197 buffer. The ADC is the real 38-pin **ADS8684AIDBTR**, not the invalid historical PWR suffix. It must be configured for 0–10.24 V acquisition and calibrated; actual firmware and loaded acquisition/freshness are unfinished. Current mode inserts four parallel 1 kΩ/1 W resistors through a normally open TLP3640A. Mode selection defaults to voltage and is gated by actual rail observations and independently latched ACC clear. The INA measures downstream of the bidirectional TBU protector, excluding its voltage drop from current sensing. Photorelay on resistance and divider/reference error still require calibration.

The TBU's 50–100 mA trigger range is guaranteed only at 25 °C. Its 1 µs figure means reduction below trigger, not immediate 1 mA leakage. The published 1 mA maximum blocking current applies at 50 V. Temperature trigger bounds, initial resistor pulse energy, reset behavior at a biased 24 V loop, repeated fault cycling and EMC/cable tests remain engineering/prototype gates. The ±30 V input-terminal protection target therefore remains unqualified; high common-mode rejection is not an arbitrary-voltage or fault-energy guarantee. A 60 V differential fault initially dissipates 3.6 W in each burden resistor until protection operates.

Three purchased isolated converter modules supply real 5 V, 15 V and 24 V rails. Their output returns intentionally join AI_AGND; LOGIC_GND remains isolated. The RECOM converter requires a 20% minimum load. Its 787 Ω/2.5 W resistor is **upstream** of the sensor limiter and guarantees at least 29.51 mA at the audited voltage/resistor corners. The sensor MAX17612C uses actual latch-off mode and 3.24 kΩ programming. The static current bounds are 87.656–97.564 mA, and **87 mA external load** plus downstream bleed and telemetry fits the minimum. Four 20 mA loops fit. The static ceiling stays below 100 mA. The 36–44 ms overcurrent blanking interval is separate from the motion watchdog. Converter thermal derating, hard-short behavior and startup are not physically proved.

AI_REF_10V is an actual OPA197 terminal-feedback driver with an inline LT3092IST limiter. Its pin 1 is SET, pin 2 and tab are OUT, and pin 3 is IN. The current-program resistors return to the terminal, not AI_AGND. Nominal current is 9.32 mA including SET current; the conservative full-corner ceiling is 9.626 mA. The real minimum-load bleed and divider/telemetry consumption leave a 7.6 mA external allocation. Its nominal voltage is 10 V. The composite feedback loop operates in limiter dropout at light loads; startup, no load, short, capacitive load and recovery stability remain prototype gates. No arbitrary ±30 V fault claim applies to this reference terminal.

An isolated ADS1115 at address 0x4A reads the actual reference terminal, protected sensor terminal, sensor SETI and 15 V rail. This is slow diagnostics, distinct from the four-channel 1 kSPS target. The mode expander is at 0x20 and existing AO telemetry remains at 0x48/49. The shared host I2C pullups are not duplicated. Absent or stale readings must remain invalid, not zero or healthy.

Parent-owned ordinary terminals and chassis coupling connect the canonical boundary names. This source contains no chassis capacitor, DC chassis bond, custom daughterboard or placeholder connector. Parent routing must enforce return domains, creepage, 10.2 mm converter height, TBU no-underpad routing and thermal copper, reference Kelvin routing, reference capacitor effective value and assembly stencil qualification. Primary MAX17612 land/package PDFs were copied from the independently reviewed safety-sensor source; LT3092 electrical/pin review used the manufacturer's web-readable Rev D because direct PDF downloads timed out. The one-page Mouser access-denied document is explicitly rejected as a datasheet.
