APPLICATIONS
• X1, Y2 according to IEC 60384-14
• Line-to-line filtering (Class X)
• Line-to-ground filtering (Class Y)
• Primary and secondary coupling (SMPS)
• EMI / RFI suppression and filtering
DESIGN
The capacitor consists of a ceramic disc which is silver 
plated on both sides. Connection leads are made of tin 
plated copper-clad steel having a diameter of 0.6 mm. 
The capacitors may be supplied with vertical (inline) kinked 
leads having a lead spacing of 5.0 mm, 7.5 mm, 10.0 mm, or 
12.5 mm. Encapsulation is made of flame retardant epoxy 
resin in accordance with UL 94 V-0.
