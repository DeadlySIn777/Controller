# Actual motherboard power-entry source

This is a 220-footprint source block on the one custom motherboard, namespace 11xxx. It supplies the existing FPGA/logic regulators rather than adding replacements for their functions. It includes three fused/protected 24 V entries, actual CORE/MOTION/FIELD buck circuits, two receiving-motor supply switches, the commodity-compute feed, separate AUX limiters and the two existing output-bank field logic supplies. The three fitted blade fuses are separate assembly accessories in `fitted-accessories-bom.csv`, in addition to the 220 PCB components.

Native KiCad ERC has zero errors and warnings. The independent exported-netlist inventory has 738 pin assignments and 42 NC pins, including the two internal nonphysical source boundary symbols. Native footprint inspection loads all 220 fitted components and 707 numbered copper pads. These checks establish source correspondence. This block has no PCB routing or fabrication release, and does not confer whole-board DRC or measured behavior.

The new series NO contacts physically interpose the final motor gate independently of the ISO7720 receiver output. A separate contact pair clears each bank-feed latch; fault withdrawal does not rely on waiting for rail capacitance to discharge. The latch lives on MOTION_5V_RAW, accepts a restored deliberate OPI rearm edge, and retains asynchronous fault clear. The parent must change only U6042 pin 7 to MOTOR_TX_GATE_SOURCE_A and pin 3 to MOTOR_TX_GATE_SOURCE_B; every other original final-gate consumer remains on MOTOR_TX_GATE_A/B. The complete exact endpoint ledger is in the validation report. Raw field health is exported from U9003/U9004 pin 12 before isolation, without changing the frozen 9xxx source.

CORE_5V and MOTION_5V_RAW are fixed 5 V converters with 4.95–5.05 V regulation before distribution losses. SBC_5V is the staged commodity-compute branch, with a planning cap of 3 A pending profile/inrush qualification. FIELD_5V and AUX rails remain in FIELD_GND; output bank B logic remains in FIELD_B_0V. No field return is inferred to be LOGIC_GND. Both motor AUX banks are supplied from the protected field A supply, as inherited. This source does not claim the external field power sources provide galvanic isolation unless the actual selected supplies and wiring provide it.

TPS26624 AUX24 A uses the published 26.7 kΩ overload testpoint (0.237–0.257 A); B uses 44.2 kΩ (0.145–0.159 A). The manufacturer's exact sustained-short maximum is stated at 7.5 kΩ, so it is not silently extrapolated to these resistances. Resistor drift and any untabulated limit remain explicit source questions. The fitted TPS7A1633 field regulators carry a 20 mA reservation per bank; their 100 mA rating does not authorize that current continuously from 30 V at 55 °C ambient.

The conditional NO LED current screen includes two series LEDs, 120 Ω 1%/100 ppm resistors and the stated driver testpoint, giving approximately 5.35–19.00 mA. Contact optical current/timing limits are guaranteed only at their listed 25 °C conditions. Driver leakage is guaranteed at the exact powered/unpowered testpoints. Arbitrary source-domain ramps below the driver's recommended VCC, raw-health input slew, healthy-at-power-up latch sequencing, full-temperature contact response, and the complete 20 ms withdrawal path remain engineering gates. They are not described as closed by clean ERC.

Remaining integration work includes the aggregate IO_3V3 current and effective capacitance/startup envelope; regulator compensation/stability and host/NVMe/ESP/display allocations; motor/AUX rail IR drop and noise; the narrower 19–29 V guaranteed static arming interval versus the inherited 18–30 V operating requirement; surge/negative-output clamp and fuse coordination; exact connector/socket mechanics; and the two-stage feed/health/rearm software sequence. These are identified electrical/integration questions, in addition to later physical prototype tests.

Primary package geometry is retained in `footprint-review.json`, with source vectors and rendered datasheet pages in `reports/motherboard-power-entry-20260930/sources`. The small asymmetric Coilcraft land follows the annotated upper 4.20 mm/lower 4.25 mm spans. Shared VIN/SW lands remain actual common copper; logical pin lands are not fake NC pads. Copper, mask, paste and assembly thermal process still need a board-level review.
