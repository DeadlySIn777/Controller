# Four-ABZ, frequency and MPG receiver source review

30 September 2026. This is **source/native circuit evidence for a soldered motherboard region**, not a custom daughterboard or manufacturing package. The exact [schematic/model/BOM](../../hardware/motherboard_fast_inputs/README.md) uses sixteen differential paths on four AM26LV32EIPWR receivers. Native KiCad ERC/XML, independent normative pin checks and scoped host mutations are recorded separately from unperformed PCB/target/physical qualification.

## Primary source and package checks

`sources/am26lv32e-reve.pdf` is TI SLLS849E (August 2023; package addendum dated November 2025), downloaded from [TI](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/am26lv32e.pdf). SHA-256: `2e3c8f022c192972982a712da7b04f96dd181caf854a420ae59aa670b9798618`. Pages 3/4/9/25/26 are rendered locally; the pin and land drawings were visually inspected. PW16 is the selected orderable package, not RGY VQFN. Independent pin triples (A, B, Y) are `(2,1,3)`, `(6,7,5)`, `(10,9,11)`, `(14,15,13)`; pin 4 G, pin 12 /G, pin 8 GND, pin 16 VCC. All four channels are used on every package, so no receiver output is marked NC.

The receiver's operating supply is 3.0–3.6 V. The differential/common-mode bounds are ±7 V; these are receiver interface specifications, not permission to apply 12/24 V field signals or an operating interpretation of absolute maximum values. Pin 4 is held high on IO_3V3, so pin 12 high does not disable the outputs. The datasheet's open-input behavior does not cover guaranteed shorted/terminated-disconnected cable behavior. Device-level ESD ratings do not prove board surge/EMC immunity. External line protection and cable fault containment remain open tasks.

`inspect_footprint.py`, run with native pcbnew, verifies all sixteen stock KiCad pad numbers/centers/pitch and records the exact library hash in [footprint-review.json](footprint-review.json). Stock `Package_SO:TSSOP-16_4.4x5mm_P0.65mm` has 0.65 mm pitch, lands **1.475 × 0.4 mm at x = ±2.8625 mm**, pin 1 top-left/pin 16 top-right. TI's example is **1.5 × 0.45 mm at x = ±2.9 mm**. Accounting for corner radii, the stock land area is about 13.59% smaller. TI's body maximum is 4.5 × 5.1 mm, height 1.2 mm. The reviewed stock footprint is retained as a prototype candidate; it is not described as identical to TI's land example or assembly-process approved. Fabricator/stencil/assembly approval and placement/courtyard context remain open.

Each chip has a 100 nF local ceramic bypass and 1 µF local bulk between IO_3V3 and LOGIC_GND; schematic connectivity does not prove physical placement/loop area. Exact candidates are GRM188R72A104KA35D and Würth 885012206052. The first is the already source-reviewed active 100 V bypass in the MC substitution ledger; the second is the existing 16 V, X7R, 0603 bulk candidate. Their purchasing/derating/drawing qualification remains a component/assembly gate; the baseline two-encoder block's unnamed caps are not silently relabelled.

## Loads, terminations and boundary truth

`sources/vishay-crcw-20035.pdf` is the manufacturer [CRCW document](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20035/dcrcwe3.pdf), downloaded revision 14 April 2026. SHA-256: `821c7a0fe3d3129c63282538b88036b730697aab4972d8c3cf1ee2cb8be6f427`. Exact termination candidate CRCW1210120RFKEA is 120 ohms, 1%, 1210, 0.5 W at 70 °C. The inherited conservative ±100 ppm/K over 150 K gives minimum resistance `120 × 0.99 × 0.985 = 117.018 ohms`; at the declared 5.5 V maximum differential, worst resistor dissipation is **0.25851 W/channel**, **3.61910 W for the fourteen fitted positions**, or **4.13612 W if all sixteen were later qualified/fitted**. Film/PCB temperature, enclosure cooling, end-of-life change, source driving current and sustained-fault power require physical/design review; nominal wattage alone is not a whole-board thermal pass.

ENC0–3's twelve signal pairs and FREQ0/1 have fitted 120-ohm terminations. Exact source/cable profiles still have to support those loads. MPG defaults to **DNP/open R15/R16**: the inherited single-ended mode's 2.5 V reference has a 1 mA limit; 2.5/120 gives about 20.8 mA. A differential MPG profile can define a separately qualified fitted termination, while a TTL profile must validate its physical reference and all loading. No software setting fits a resistor or upgrades a rail rating.

`B1`–`B4` preserve the ten-position XE0–3 function order; `B5/B6` preserve XF/XH1; `B7` is the external protected-feed/reference/chassis handoff; `B8` is IO_3V3/ground/16 FPGA outputs; `B9` hands off accessory ID/presence and MPG-enable to **not-yet-built** frontend regions. All nine symbols are nonphysical and excluded from board/BOM. They cannot appear in CPL or be mistaken for the final connectors. Parent-provided supplies are ENC0–3 protected 5 V, FREQ protected 5 V and MPG protected 5 V, with distinct return/chassis. This region does not supply the port limiters, 2.5 V reference, isolation, identity bridge, presence/MPG status acquisition, pendant, probes or FPGA ball assignment.
