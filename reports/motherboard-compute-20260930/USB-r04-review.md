# Native compute carrier r04: protected USB host and recovery

30 September 2026. This is a reviewed native electrical source extension, not PCB routing, USB certification, target execution or a manufacturing release.

The compute region now has **142 physical footprints, 134 purchased fitted parts and eight bare test pads**. It adds 45 physical parts to the previous 97-part SD/HDMI carrier. Native KiCad ERC reports zero errors, zero warnings and no ignored checks. Independent validation checks all **767 native pins and 247 nets**, exact component/pad inventories and the separate signal/power domains. All 44 disconnected mutation tests pass. The original 97 fitted MPNs and footprints remain identical. Exactly three genuinely NC socket contacts become real Pi diagnostics: GPIO5/contact34 for USB fault, GPIO3/contact56 for SCL and GPIO2/contact58 for SDA. Their literal old/new native names are in `r04-exact-native-rename-ledger.json`; no generic rename exception is authorized.

One Wurth 614004134726 USB-A connector supplies the host. A separate GCT USB4105-GF-A provides USB2 recovery with two real 5.1 kΩ CC pull-downs. Recovery VBUS has a bleed, ESD device and test pad and does not supply SBC_5V, module AUX, IO3 or host VBUS. Its actual sixteen contacts, overlapping manufacturer common-power/ground lands, four shell stakes and two mechanical anchors are preserved. Both connector metals connect to the explicit CHASSIS net. The root board must supply the actual local ESD-return/chassis coupling and qualified panel bond.

FSUSB42MUX uses its **MSOP10** pin assignment, not the differently numbered UQFN device. The cold selector sets host/recovery, connect/disconnect and the module boot strap. Actual module-supervised rail qualification and hardware logic disconnect data and disable host power without the qualified module. The controls have real inactive defaults. The OTG_ID buffer runs from the module's own AUX rail and has specified powered-off protection. Cold role selection is a circuit requirement; live switching and target USB drivers are not qualified.

TPS2553DBVR supplies the sole USB-A through a 60.4 kΩ ±1% ILIM resistor. The datasheet equations give **0.387252–0.491222 A** worst-case limiting. The planned normal bank draw is 350 mA, including its 5 mA discharge bleed, leaving **345 mA** for a qualified external load. This is not a guarantee that a standard 500 mA device can draw 500 mA. Powered, customer-sourceable USB hubs can provide qualified expansion. There is no USB-IF compliance claim.

The actual 0.1 Ω WSL0805R1000FEA shunt feeds INA219AIDR with the **SOIC8** pin map, actual own-AUX supply and 0x40 address straps. Its manufacturer's two current lands have a 0.50 mm gap and must be Kelvin-routed. The current register requires software calibration; raw ADC availability is not target driver evidence. GPIO5 now observes the real open-drain TPS2553 fault. Neither diagnostic grants motion permission.

The 330 µF / 10 V Panasonic EEEFK1A331P is **F case: 8 × 10.2 mm**, not the smaller E case used by a different capacitance/voltage variant. Its exact manufacturer gap/land dimensions are 3.1 mm / 4.0 × 2.0 mm. The 264 µF initial lower tolerance bound exceeds 120 µF; cumulative reflow/age, low-temperature impedance, startup ramp, hotplug, cable drop and capacitive fault recovery still require actual qualification. No simulated capacitor solid or invented mechanical clearance is provided.

The module AUX planning reservation becomes **0.425 A**. Pi CM4/CM5 explicitly allow 0.6 A total, but BTT auxiliary-current and these new GPIO transport functions remain unqualified. The complete 3 A SBC simultaneous CM allowance, USB current maximum, AUX conversion and HDMI fault reserve is still **not closed**. The six 5 V contacts' 0.3 A blanket simultaneous-contact rating does not establish a 2.5 A socket guarantee. The source and receipt retain these limits openly.
