Series 219 
SMD DIP Switch                                                  
 Removable tape seal to withstand IR vapor phase or     
wave soldering  temperatures, and board washing 
 Gull-wing and “J”bend terminal configurations 
 Available low profile, medium, and extended height 
actuators      
 SPST configuration available 
 0.6mm/.024” actuator travel      
 Optional top tape seal for board spray washing 
 AEC-Q200 Certification 
 
 
Description 
Positive detent separated from contactor causes contactor does not deflect during actuation. Unique compact 
type design allows to be used at small size application. Optional top tape sealed structure is optimized for board 
washing during soldering process. It makes it the ideal choice for any server, security and HVAC systems.
