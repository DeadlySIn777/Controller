# Independent review of frozen wide-input AI/AO source r02

The exact owner receipt is copied as `reviewed-r02-source-receipt.json`; matching native inputs and manufacturer evidence are hashed in `receipt.json`. All 554 AI and 474 AO native pins independently agree with their physical source models. Five actual converters use the reviewed 9–36 V TMR4-WI source and correct seven-terminal SIP8 mapping. This repairs the protected-entry minimum mismatch at the product's 18 V input without changing the external voltage requirement or adding another board.

The AI 5 V source is now an isolated 12 V converter followed by fixed TPS7A2650DRVR. The TPS3702CX50DDCR's actual SET HIGH window admits the conservative 4.92–5.08 V regulator screen. Its full-temperature limits leave only 6.8 mV at the ADC's lower accuracy boundary and 3.2 mV at the upper boundary; Kelvin sense at ADC AVDD/AGND, distributed return drop and transient/propagation behavior remain mandatory physical checks. A monitor cannot make an arbitrarily fast collapsing rail instantaneously valid. Samples from a fault interval require rejection under the eventual target validity/freshness contract.

U14705/U14706 now receive VDD and local bypass from regulated AI5, closing the prior actual 24 V TPS3700 supply violation. The 15/24 V dividers are coarse rail-health checks, distinct from ADC supply accuracy. R14706 is now 332 kΩ: the previous 344 kΩ release corner had negligible normal margin. The final independent 100 K resistor excursion calculation includes comparator thresholds, opposite resistor tolerance/TCR and published input bias. Both coarse windows admit the stated converter bands; hysteresis is already included in the published positive/negative thresholds and is not added twice.

The actual 4 MHz AI transport, worst resistor LED paths and conditional manufacturer tables total a **94.19 mA** current screen. ADC digital current and several loaded/mixed-supply conditions lack a guaranteed complete maximum. A **110 mA planning reservation** yields about **0.833 W** LDO loss and **116.1°C** junction at 55°C ambient using the JEDEC 73.3°C/W reference. This is credible source sizing, not a thermal measurement or a 500 mA board claim. The actual WSON exposed pad, ground copper, effective output capacitance of at least 1 µF and startup/fault recovery must qualify this profile.

Preserving prior AO/AI reservations and adding actual 12 V/LDO loss gives about **7.875 W** of analog output-side reservations. Five newly fitted 4 W modules do not promise 20 W of continuous controller capability. Their no-load/full-load currents, efficiency and startup data are typical; shared protected 24 V input, foldback and thermal checks remain distinct physical gates. Normal operation must fit; operation through simultaneous shorts is not promised. Safe inhibition and fault recovery must remain correct.

The owner native ERC results are zero errors and zero warnings for both blocks. This review independently checks native pins and manufacturer/corner reasoning; it does not rerun the owner's full mutation tests, modify their frozen source, simulate regulator dynamics or claim board routing/manufacturing readiness.
