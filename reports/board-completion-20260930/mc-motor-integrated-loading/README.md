# Independent integrated motion-card loading review

30 September 2026, `mc-motor-integrated-loading-r01`. This is a **read-only review of the 327-part connected motion-card source**, with eight standard and four expansion motor channels. The reviewed schematic, native netlist, source ledgers and primary datasheets are copied and hashed in [input-manifest.json](input-manifest.json). [analysis.json](analysis.json) retains every inspected motor GPIO/output node, shared permission fanout, direct I/O-rail capacitor and numerical screen. [analyze.py](analyze.py) imports no hardware generator or validator. It checks 1,123 declared connected pins against the native export, compares all conductor assignments and native pin functions with a **fresh KiCad 10.0.4 export of the copied six-sheet source**, and observes zero violations in its copied native ERC report. This is source/native-export inspection and arithmetic, without new target compilation, simulation, PCB DRC or physical measurements.

No circuit, published board, prior receipt or frozen IS-1 file was changed. All 38 source inputs remained byte-identical during this review. The earlier 203-part review and the standalone motor-buffer review retain their original evidence boundaries.

## Findings that prevent an electrical release

1. **The qualified bank-B output-enable driver is not implemented.** The actual `BANK_B_BUFFER_ENABLE_QUALIFIED` copper joins all 13 used positive-OE inputs and U4013.6, the OPI_BOE asynchronous clear. That is the required observation relationship. The same net contains R4014 10 kΩ and R5076 4.7 kΩ pulldowns, with no fitted output/bidirectional pin or FPGA ball. Its default remains LOW; the expansion outputs cannot operate from this schematic alone. The missing upstream qualification must supply this actual net, retain the OPI clear relationship and be checked for glitches, loaded levels and partial power. A software-request alias cannot substitute for the actual physical OE signal.
2. **The proposed 0.50 A motion-card slot allocation is not closed by the integrated load.** The power block's 0.467353 A baseline is an allocation model using 0.8 A FPGA core, 0.15 A FPGA I/O, 85% efficiency, 50 mA overhead per regulator and 10 mA other supervision. It has no assigned, demonstrated allowance for this entire new loaded motor stage. Preserving that baseline and adding the clearly itemized stage allocation below gives 0.509500 A before additional CMOS input current, and 0.621348 A with the conditional full-125°C input-current allocation. The corresponding concurrent-startup screen is 0.846044 A against the proposed 0.80 A startup allowance. Dynamic switching, cold input-capacitor charging and several support/receiver loads remain unbounded. These numbers require a versioned budget/branch/connector/thermal review; they do not authorize changing a limiter or frozen slot allocation.
3. **The declared full-125°C bank-A active-LOW enable voltage screen has no positive LOW margin.** Its required receiving-card 10 kΩ pull-up plus receiving-input leakage allocates 355 µA sink current. This exceeds the TI244A 100 µA light-load test row. The 3 V/24 mA full-125°C VOL limit is 0.8 V; the 33 Ω +1% series allowance adds 11.832 mV, producing 0.811832 V against the receiving LVC07A 0.8 V VIL. This is a source-only qualification deficit, not a measured failure at 355 µA. Interpolating the guaranteed test row, or applying the 2.7 V row at a different supply, needs justification. The ≤85°C 3 V loaded row gives 0.561832 V including series drop. Frozen IS-1 specifies 0–55°C internal ambient, so the intended temperature/load scope may close this issue after actual local temperature and loaded waveform qualification; the part's 125°C rating alone is insufficient evidence. No component substitution is made here.

## Actual pull and fanout inventory

**There are no retained core 10 kΩ pulls in parallel with the 36 motor GPIO inputs.** Each native STEP/DIR input has one 4.7 kΩ pulldown, and each EN_N input has one 4.7 kΩ pull-up to the MC I/O rail. The earlier reserved enable GPIO names are now aliases of the actual EN_N inputs. All 36 inputs contain exactly one FPGA pin, one buffer input and the default resistor.

The two actual parallel-pull cases are permission nets. Their native inventories differ materially from the standalone motor block's external fanout assumptions:

| Actual net | Fitted defaults and receivers | Conditional HIGH allocation at 3.465 V | Conditional LOW allocation |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `TX_PERMIT` | 10 kΩ + 4.7 kΩ PD; FPGA E6; three permission inputs; one 244A A and one 2G126 A | 1.144681 mA, including 50 µA receiving leakage | 178.333 µA with FPGA configuration weak PU |
| `BANK_B_BUFFER_ENABLE_QUALIFIED` | 10 kΩ + 4.7 kΩ PD; 13 OE inputs + U4013.6; driver absent | 1.164681 mA, including 70 µA receiving leakage | Floating current-only screen 0.226048 V |
| `FPGA_CRESET_N` | Power block's own 4.7 kΩ PD, external 10 kΩ PD, four LVC input pins; no direct FPGA pad | External 370 µA, within the power block's 1 mA boundary | External 20 µA, within its 100 µA boundary |

The reset external contract excludes its own fitted 4.7 kΩ. Including that resistor and the external receivers gives 1.101602 mA at the power block's stated 3.4 V/2% pull screen, within the cited loaded buffer row. It is not a reset-boundary overload. Future FPGA BOE readback, field monitoring or cable loads need explicit additions; none is invented as a fitted input here. TX_PERMIT's configuration LOW load exceeds 100 µA, so its driver cannot be described using the light-load 0.1 V guarantee. The ordinary loaded LVC row is the applicable conditional screen.

The STEP/DIR configuration divider screen includes the FPGA 27 kΩ minimum weak pull-up, 4.7 kΩ +1% pulldown, and allocated buffer/FPGA leakage. Its worst HIGH is 0.639223 V for bank A and 0.578665 V for bank B, below 0.8 V. EN_N's intended configuration pull-up helps its HIGH/disabled default. A broader adversarial user-selected 30 kΩ weak pulldown screen gives 2.588071 V / 2.649548 V, above 2.0 V. These rely on assigned leakage and valid rails; the TI input-leakage table's specified voltage points do not establish the entire intermediate-voltage/ramp behavior. The T20 drive-strength table is explicitly at nominal voltage and 25°C. FPGA mode, full loaded temperature behavior, package/bank limits and device fit remain unqualified. [Efinix T20, Tables 72–75 and 129](https://www.efinixinc.com/docs/trion20-ds-v6.0.pdf).

## Additional I/O-rail and slot allocation

Four fitted 244A ICs have **25 used data inputs**, seven grounded unused data inputs and eight grounded OE inputs. Seven fitted 2G126 ICs have **13 used data inputs and 13 used OE inputs**; the spare channel is grounded. The count comes from the native package pins, not the logical path ledger.

| Additional-stage allocation item | Current |
|---|---:|
| 36 GPIO strong pulls + conservative weak-pull + actual bank-specific input leakage | 31.968511 mA |
| 26 fitted outgoing HIGH pulldowns | 9.100000 mA |
| 26 required future receiving STEP/DIR/TX HIGH pulldowns | 9.100000 mA |
| 24 required future AM26C31I input leakage allowances | 0.024000 mA |
| Newly added TX/BOE 4.7 kΩ pulls and their new fitted receiving inputs | 1.579362 mA |
| Eleven buffers' rail-input, unloaded ICC only | 0.230000 mA |
| **Additive allocation before nonrail input/dynamic current** | **52.001872 mA** |

The standalone 32.148511 mA GPIO figure conservatively used 20 µA for every motor buffer input. Actual bank-specific leakage reduces that arithmetic by 0.180 mA; this is not a meaningful cost or power-budget solution. Receiver circuits and the BOE driver are absent, so their necessary assumptions are visibly separate from fitted observations. TX receiver leakage/fanout, other support logic and qualified-driver supply current still need allocation. The FPGA 0.15 A reservation must be assigned by circuit, state and operating envelope before any overlapping allowance can be removed. Adding a load twice is avoidable; assuming that an unspecified reservation already covers it is not evidence.

The TI244A 125°C additional-input-current row is **5 mA for one input at VCC−0.6 V, with all other inputs at rails**. Multiplying this point by its 25 used inputs gives a **conditional allocation of 125 mA**, rather than blindly multiplying every physical pin. The 2G126 analogous row contributes 26 ×0.5 mA = **13 mA**, yielding **138 mA conditional additional current**. This is neither a guaranteed simultaneous-input bound nor a bound for arbitrary startup voltages. A LOW node below VIL is still not a rail for an ICC test. At the ≤85°C 244A row the analogous combined allocation is 25.5 mA. Thermal scope and input waveforms therefore matter to the final budget. The 0.230 mA unloaded rail-input ICC and 32.149 mA GPIO resistor screen cannot be reported as complete operating supply current. [TI244A electrical characteristics](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc244a.pdf), [TI2G126 electrical characteristics](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g126.pdf).

For the preserved baseline allocation, the full-125°C conditional motor-stage addition is **190.001872 mA on IO_3V3**, raising the unqualified I/O reservation study to 340.001872 mA. Conversion to its 5 V slot screen uses 3.34125/(4.85 ×0.85) = 0.810491. The earlier quoted 0.467353 A slot number must not simply have 32.149 mA added to it: currents at different rails need conversion, and output loads/input supply current need inclusion. The new 0.621348/0.846044 A screens are lower-level planning evidence, not new agreed limits or complete worst cases. The regulator/inductor, entry/branch protection, connector/copper, startup coordination and enclosure thermal model must be reconciled at the final configured FPGA workload before ordering.

**Active-LOW enable sink current comes from the receiver's independently powered pull-up rail.** Its 355 µA per enabled channel contributes load to that receiver's supply and heat in the MC output transistor; it is not added again as 355 µA sourced by the MC 3.3 V regulator. Remote receiver IC quiescent and switching current belong to the remote supply budget. Ground-return integrity and the MCU/FPGA/differential-card power states remain part of whole-system qualification.

## Bypass capacitance and partial power

The 11 newly fitted 100 nF motor capacitors add **1.1 µF nominal / 1.3915 µF tolerance-temperature upper screen**. Across all new supervision blocks, direct MC I/O bypass capacitance is 0.7 µF watchdog +1.9 µF permission +1.1 µF motor = **3.7 µF nominal**, within the power block's already reserved 10 µF additional load. The populated direct IO_3V3 upper total is **81.93515 µF**, while the preserved power model using the entire reservation is **89.90465 µF**, below its 100 µF effective-capacitance ceiling. The 1.1 µF is not added a second time to the already reserved 89.90465 µF. DC-bias minimum capacitance, loaded ramp, indirect RC charging and lifetime remain distinct open checks.

There is correctly **no outgoing EN_N pull-up to the MC rail**. Each receiving card must provide its own 10 kΩ +1% pull-up to its independent, valid 3.14–3.465 V rail. At the Ioff test condition of exactly zero MC buffer VCC, including allocated receiving leakage and a conservative series allowance, EN_N remains ≥2.886833 V in bank A /2.988167 V in bank B; STEP/DIR remains ≤0.212767/0.111433 V. These are useful conditional inactive-state margins. Ioff does not prove arbitrary rail ramps, opposite partial power with MC on and AM26C31 receiver off, or FPGA core-off/I/O-on behavior. The missing receiving circuits must actually preserve these biases. The AM26C31 receiving part is not assumed to have an unlisted Ioff guarantee. [TI AM26C31](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/am26c31.pdf), [TI LVC07A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc07a.pdf).

Bank-A STEP/DIR LOW has no remote HIGH pull-up, so the small receiving leakage can use the 100 µA row: its conditional LOW including series is 0.300033 V, and its HIGH with two pulldowns is 2.226636 V from the cited 3 V/12 mA row. The bank-A EN_N issue must not be generalized into an invented failure on every STEP/DIR output. Bank-B EN_N's loaded row gives 0.411832 V, with 0.388168 V margin against the selected receiving threshold. Full supply/load/temperature/edge applicability still needs physical closure.

## Retained 20 ms requirement

The preserved 3.3 nF watchdog study allocates 13.131452 ms to the extended timer corner and 4.31 ms downstream, totalling **17.441452 ms**, leaving **2.558548 ms** below 20 ms under its stated assumptions. The new buffers' datasheet nanosecond test-condition delays alone do not consume that millisecond allowance, and no new numerical 20 ms failure is asserted here. However, the existing report explicitly leaves startup/SET1 maximum evaluation, complete permission-chain delays, CWD leakage/environment, actual load capacitance and physical clock-stall response unproved. Its self-test SET1 setup uses a 0.15 ms nominal value without a guaranteed maximum.

The connected 327-part source still lacks the complete qualified bank-B driver, receiving MIA/MX4 shutdown/enable circuits, field PERMIT_OUT stage and the final machine-linked inhibit path. The full physical fault-to-inhibit claim therefore cannot be established by this schematic, its zero ERC, or ideal digital checks. Preserve 20 ms and the 3.3 nF candidate; close an actual source-to-receiver timing chain and instrumented fault test. Any changed full-path budget or qualification interface requires an explicit proposed revision and logic-owner agreement, without changing frozen IS-1. This is an electrical completion gate, not a reason to label the existing evidence invalid or to restart the design.

## Reproduction and evidence boundary

Run `python -X utf8 reports/board-completion-20260930/mc-motor-integrated-loading/analyze.py`. It verifies its immutable input hashes, compares the retained fresh native conductor export with source, checks the actual pull/fanout and capacitor inventory, and reproduces the arithmetic. To regenerate the fresh export, run KiCad `sch export netlist --format kicadxml --output <this folder>/fresh-netlist.xml <this folder>/inputs/hardware/motion_card/motion_card.kicad_sch`; all five child schematics are retained beside the copied parent. The helper does not call shared generators or overwrite shared source. [review.json](review.json) identifies the independent helper/results/report hashes. The source can be a useful constrained engineering prototype package; **neither this review nor the prior test counts make it a complete functioning twelve-channel controller or a PCBWay release**.
