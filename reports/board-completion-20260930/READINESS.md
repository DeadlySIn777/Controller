## One genuine decision needed before physical integration can be frozen

The newer user instruction requires **one custom PCB with purchased drop-in modules** and prohibits inventing custom daughterboard families. The same completion instruction also requires preserving the **frozen specification**. Frozen [IS-1 section02](C:/Users/Gluis/claude-worktrees/controller-interface-spec/docs/interface-spec/02-modules-and-boundaries.md), D02-1/2/3 and section4, requires13 custom-card positions on a passive base, standing cards, fixed machine terminals and designated removable FPGA/I/O/expansion cards. Those physical requirements cannot simultaneously describe one active custom motherboard without changing the service/expansion contract.

The compute-bay choice does not settle that conflict. No proven purchased FPGA/expansion/cartridge combination presently preserves all the frozen service boundaries. The pending clarification is which physical requirement takes precedence. It is not approval to order, a request for general architectural alternatives, or a claim that one motherboard cannot implement the logical functions. [Feasibility review](one-pcb-feasibility/README.md) provides the concrete functional map and exact conflict. Earlier frozen files remain untouched.
