## Physical qualification after the necessary design exists

P1: measure complete voltage/current/thermal limits, startup/inrush/sequence, partial-power/defaults, shorts/reverse/transients, inductive switching, isolation and calibrated diagnostics. P2: prove target 1 kHz exchange under graphics/storage/network/accessory load, the 700 µs engineering target and ≤20 ms fault-to-inhibit for covered faults; inject clock/link/reset/brownout/interlock faults and prove deliberate recovery. P3: complete the specified 72-hour combined-load run with motor and encoder expansion together; validate all channels, EMC/environment/mechanics, service exchange and interrupted-write recovery. Screen/touch/compute/enclosure packages require separate qualified records. These measurements cannot substitute for E1–E8.
