# One custom motherboard: consolidation feasibility

30 September 2026. **Read-only source inspection and arithmetic.** This report does not modify frozen IS-1, the circuit sources, machine profiles, public configuration schema or PCB revisions. It does not establish a finished motherboard, target FPGA fit, production cost or manufacture readiness. The accompanying `inventory.json` identifies the inspected inputs by SHA-256.

The current motion and fault architecture can be implemented as functional regions on one custom motherboard with purchased compute and ESP modules. SPI, hardware watchdogs, permission latches, isolated field interfaces and freshness-checked I/O do not intrinsically require separate custom PCBs. The current source does **not** prove the complete design fits, routes, powers or runs on one motherboard. A new physical contract must replace the frozen removable-card contract if the latest one-PCB instruction takes precedence.

## What is actually present

The current `hardware/motion_card/design.json` contains **327 physical component candidates**: 70 FPGA-core parts, 48 local-power parts, 29 watchdog-conditioning parts, 56 permission parts and 124 motor/TX-buffer parts. It is an eight-standard/twelve-total motor **logic-side** candidate. The 38 buffered outgoing paths are 12 STEP, 12 DIR, 12 individual EN_N and two TX permits. They are not 12 complete drive interfaces. Its source explicitly says `interface_agreed: false`, `manufacturing_ready: false` and `eight_twelve_runtime_qualified: false`.

The separate `hardware/controller_assembly/` contains **13 connected schematic blocks and 570 exported components**, rather than 13 fully finished IS-1 service cards. Its recorded validation reports 240 unselected/boundary parts and 126 unassigned footprints; those two numbers are recorded validator results, not a fresh production-BOM audit here. Its native export is a partial controller: old two-entry power, 12 differential STEP/DIR transmitter paths, two encoder channels, 22 historical direct isolated inputs, the old single-watchdog permission block and an ESP service circuit. It omits several frozen interfaces and contains superseded topology. The MC and this assembly must be reconciled by function; their component counts must not be added together.

## Functional contracts that can survive consolidation

- Keep the local real-time Linux → FPGA division. The workstation, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, optional AI and service UI remain outside hard motion timing. The internal CNC3 ABI/dual-CS sequence can be retained; Ethernet remains a user/accessory/AI interface as assigned, not a replacement for a proven local transport merely because Ethernet is exposed.
- Keep two independently supervised progress streams, fail-closed rail/reset/permission logic, individual motor enables/alarms, explicit arm/self-test/recovery and hardware output inhibits. Preserve the ≤20 ms fault-to-inhibit requirement and absence of automatic resume.
- Keep IOL1 as four local FPGA-to-MCU links. Its class/slot/epoch/CRC/freshness checks, 1 ms accepted-frame timer, independent MCU watchdogs and output readback do not depend on card connectors. Retain electrical isolation across the actual field barriers. Any revised identity object must honestly distinguish soldered functional regions from removable modules.
- Keep loaded 1 kHz read/compute/write acceptance, the 700 µs engineering target, diagnostics validity/age, offline configuration/recovery/licensing, ordinary third-party peripherals and optional AI. Screen tiers are independent of motor/I/O count.

The principal genuine user decision is therefore **service and expansion topology**, not whether an internal SPI trace is possible. The root task has asked which conflicting physical requirement takes precedence. This report does not decide it. Component selection, rail sizing, isolation, pin allocation, terminal placement and routing are necessary engineering work under either answer.

## Resource, voltage and geometry blockers to prove

1. **FPGA pins and implementation:** frozen IS-1 allocates 175 signals against 189 usable advertised GPIO, leaving 14 arithmetical spare. Only 137 are full-feature GPIO, so at least 38 signals must use the relevant LVDS-as-GPIO capability under verified bank/voltage/direction rules. The current source has 96 allocated user-I/O entries and no assigned LVDS entries; the rest is not a fitted 175-pin design. Of the 101 frozen direct-field signal names, 41 lack exact current FPGA pin-net names; acknowledging the two historical PROBE/TOOLSETTER aliases leaves **39** still missing, including all 24 expansion encoder inputs, FREQ/MPG, PWM/PEN and safety feedback. Those aliases are an inspection aid, not new interface agreement. The full host dual-CS, output progress, IOL1/status/fast-I/O/permission allocation must also be completed and vendor-fitted before a finished layout. This is an unproven resource gate, not evidence that one PCB is impossible. Connector removal does not automatically reduce a frozen functional budget.
2. **Compute pin compatibility:** connector shape cannot make CM4, CM5, CB1 and CB2 electrically interchangeable. The existing carrier strategy records changed CM4/CM5 USB/MIPI and reset semantics, including pin 92, and separate BTT pin/software profiles. An unsupported module must not damage hardware before software can reject it. A same-motherboard upgrade promise requires one explicit compatibility matrix, safe reset/power wiring, pinmux ownership and enough power/cooling for every advertised upgrade. Separate incompatible carrier revisions do not meet the latest “mainboard stays” promise.
3. **Power:** frozen allocation separates CORE5 (6 A) from MOTION5 (≥3 A), and EX8 has its own converter. It reserves 4.5 A CORE5, 2.346 A MOTION5 with twelve motors, and 3.011 A minimum-input CORE24 loading with twelve encoders; its 4 A-class entry replaces the old 3 A core-entry study. FIELD_A and FIELD_B continuous allocations are 4.578 A and 4.82 A. These are requirements/arithmetic, not measured ratings. Consolidation must keep their fault containment and cannot reuse the historical single-rail 6 A study as a complete expanded budget. Current local MC 0.50 A steady/0.80 A startup proposal also differs from frozen 0.45 A and needs integrated reconciliation.
4. **Actual voltage domains:** FPGA/control I/O is 3.3 V, core 1.2 V; field sources are bounded nominal-24 V domains with defined protected outputs and limited isolated contact loops. Combining boards does not combine grounds across galvanic barriers or make ports “any voltage.” Fuse/eFuse, transient, reverse-current, creepage/clearance and partial-power/default-state work must use actual selected components and cable/load limits.
5. **Geometry and manufacturability:** neither 327 placed MC parts nor 570 connected schematic symbols establishes placement of all 451 terminal positions, compute socket/cooling, isolated power/analog regions, PHY/HDMI/USB, high-current entries, BGA escapes and RF/PE keep-outs. The 220×100 mm motion-card candidate must not become an assumed whole-board limit. Select a complete motherboard envelope and prove it with exact footprints, a layer/stack-up/process proposal, full physical connectivity, uncapped unconnected count, DRC, power/return paths and thermal/EMC review. No special via/process substitution is implied.
6. **Firmware and timing:** keeping the CNC3/IOL1 contract internally is feasible; current earlier six/two simulation, host tests, ideal logic checks and source ERC do not prove the complete eight/twelve motion+I/O endpoint or simultaneous graphics/target timing. Complete controller MCU firmware, flash restore, immutable approved local images and deliberate recovery before claiming plug-and-play replacement.

## Concrete next integration proof

After the physical contract is reconciled, produce one top-level motherboard design with a typed net map from every field terminal and purchased-module pin to its actual circuit consumer, rail, protection, owner and diagnostic source. Reuse verified MC circuits and valid portions of existing peripheral circuits, replacing superseded topology rather than merging both wholesale. Close the complete signal and worst-case power/startup budgets; then place and route the complete circuit, including return paths and all machine interfaces. Save native schematic/PCB/ERC/DRC and exact round-trip pad/net/copper evidence for the same immutable revision. Only then can a complete prototype manufacturing package be reviewed. Physical timing, protection, thermals, EMC and service acceptance remain subsequent measurements.

No supplier submission, fabrication export or source-architecture change was performed by this investigation.
