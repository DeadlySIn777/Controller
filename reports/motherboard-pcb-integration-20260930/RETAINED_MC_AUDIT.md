# Retained 327-part physical audit

The current fitted source contains no rack DIN/har-bus connectors, card cage, custom daughterboards, compute SoM, ESP module, compute/ESP sockets or card-ID EEPROM. The zero socket count means these interfaces are absent, not finished free parts. Exact parts and native connectivity are in `retained-mc-inventory.csv`; `retained-mc-audit.json` contains the count and source hashes. No component was removed.

The retained 327 are 70 FPGA/config/reset/clock parts, 48 local rail/sequencing parts, 29 independent watchdog-conditioning parts, 56 permission/interruption/trip-record parts and 124 motor/TX buffer/default/series/bypass parts. The other actual motherboard interfaces add source-backed circuits; legacy net strings do not describe extra manufactured boards.

The two TPS62901 regulators U2001/U2002 supply different 1.2 V and 3.3 V rails from CORE_5V. They are not duplicate per-card regulators. U2003/U2004/U2005 are LTC2962 supervisors in an explicit 5 V → 1.2 V → 3.3 V qualification/enable sequence. Consolidating those three packages could be investigated only while preserving the actual voltage windows, sequencing, reset and brownout behavior; no replacement or saving is qualified here.

U5001–U5004 provide 32 always-active Ioff buffer channels, 25 used (24 motor signals plus one TX permission). U5005–U5011 provide 14 positive-OE Ioff channels, 13 used (12 motor signals plus one TX permission). The 38 used channels are exactly twelve STEP/DIR/enable triples plus two bank permissions. U5004 uses one of eight channels and leaves seven real NC outputs; it is the clearest package-consolidation opportunity. A reviewed single-channel Ioff buffer could replace that one octal package while retaining its actual output bias, bypass, source series resistor, unpowered behavior and loading/timing. No deletion or dollar saving is claimed.

The seven positive-OE bank-B packages differ electrically from bank-A always-active packages. Same-board placement does not prove their independent permission/default/partial-power functions unnecessary. Replacing them with fewer wider buffers would require explicit OE polarity/default and interruption-latch reconciliation, not removal of the gate.

U3001/U3002 are independent actual useful-progress motion/output timers. U4005/U4006 require deliberate request edges after permission loss. U4011/U4012/U4013 record actual TX/output/buffer interruptions, and U4014–U4019 implement actual watchdog-trip records and reset/rearm qualification. Their function survives physical card consolidation. None is a card-presence EEPROM.

Names such as `*_TO_MIA` and `*_TO_MX4` on the 38 motor-buffer nets are preserved historical IDs now joined to the on-motherboard motor circuitry. They do not create MIA/MX4 boards. `DIC_OK_QUALIFIED` feeding U4001.6 must become authentic onboard DI/field health; `BANK_B_BUFFER_ENABLE_QUALIFIED` still controls fourteen physical OE pins and U4013's observation. Neither may be permanently tied healthy to erase the old card concept.

The audit also exposed a concrete command-bridge error: U4019 drives `WD_M/O_TRIP_CLEAR_N`, so the FPGA cannot drive those outputs through passive resistors. The corrected destination is the actual U4018 `WD_REARM_M/O` inputs with active-high/default-low behavior. The r01 populated board retains the invalid pre-correction bridge source and is superseded for circuit correctness. Its population/copper retention measurements remain historical geometry evidence only. A zero regular DRC or source-pin parity result did not detect that electrical mistake.
