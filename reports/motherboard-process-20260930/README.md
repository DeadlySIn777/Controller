# PWM and process source receipt

The frozen `hardware/motherboard_process/` source contains **68 purchased fitted parts, 216 unique electrical pads and 222 native pad shapes**. It implements two 5 V push-pull PWM outputs, two floating normally-open 0–30 V / 50 mA process contacts, separate hardware rearm latches and a protected 50 mA-target PWM auxiliary supply. This is circuitry on the one custom motherboard; the three boundary symbols are unfitted schematic interfaces.

Native KiCad XML matches independently reviewed pin maps, values and footprints. Native ERC reports zero errors, zero warnings and no ignored checks. Native pcbnew loads all 68 footprints and verifies every numbered pad set. Six independent vendor-land assertions cover the PMV20XNEA SOT23 and TPS1H000 DGN8 copper geometry. The native schematic PDF is a review export, not a fabrication package.

The receipt records **204 freshly passing disconnected tests**: process 35, I/O control 38, operator 43, DO 36, DI 28 and motors 24. Its 42 source/input hashes are unchanged across testing and are copied into `frozen-inputs/`. The process gate trace is an ideal logic evaluation of the actual source graph; it is neither HDL timing simulation nor a physical response measurement.

The ACC latch clears when output permission or controller reset disappears. Process permission additionally clears a separate process latch; its return cannot reenergize old process commands without a fresh strobe. Missing IO3 makes the exact SN74LVC2T45 isolate its outputs, while independent gates pulled from the local 5 V rail turn on the PWM shunt clamps. Ordinary IO3-only SN74LVC08 gates have no claimed powered-off Ioff guarantee. The LM66100 local supply blocks sustained reverse feed into the upstream motion rail under its specified conditions; reverse transient/startup behavior remains a measurement gate.

The 20 mA loaded PWM voltage calculation is a conditional screening calculation: the translator's quoted 3.8 V / 0.55 V limits are tested at both supplies 4.5 V, whereas this source uses A=3.3 V and B=5 V. Actual loaded levels, clamp transition/contention, partial-power behavior and full-temperature MOS gate behavior remain unqualified. PMV20XNEA's 2.5 V RDS(on) and gate-leakage conditions are retained; a 6 V-only threshold/RDS specification was not substituted.

TPS1H000 has an actual 9.53 kΩ, 0.1%, 25 ppm/K current-limit resistor and a delay capacitor selecting latch-off rather than retry. The nominal programmed current is 50.37 mA. Published overload accuracy gives a conditional 40.10–60.73 mA screen after the declared resistor tolerance/drift budget. **50 mA is a target, not a guaranteed maximum.** The source retains the manufacturer's minimum programmed current, voltage-drop and overload conditions. Empty auxiliary ports can produce an open-load fault and must be classified in commissioning firmware. Delay values are typical and do not prove the 20 ms total inhibit requirement.

Remaining engineering includes the real fourth ACC/IOL1 controller, its target firmware/watchdog/provisioning, actual parent permission/rail qualification, and final terminals/returns. Physical gates cover loaded PWM levels, short-circuit energy, fault timing, process-contact release, ESD/EFT/surge, power sequencing and thermal behavior. No fabricated Gerbers, routing, assembly files or manufacturing-ready claim are provided by this receipt.

Reproduction: run the source builder, KiCad schematic XML export and ERC, the independent validator, `native_footprint_review.py` with KiCad's Python, then `freeze_receipt.py` with the project Python. `receipt.json`, `input-hashes.json`, `sources/manifest.json` and the native footprint review contain the exact evidence boundaries.
