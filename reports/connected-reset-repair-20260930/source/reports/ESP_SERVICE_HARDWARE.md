# ESP32-S3 service and recovery circuit

28 September 2026. An actual editable circuit now connects the **ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8** to its existing regulated supply and independent reset sequence, with USB service, isolated-by-buffer UART directions, boot recovery and reserved internal GPIO. It provides no motion permission or implemented accessory actuation. This is a schematic candidate, not a production board.

## Module, power and reset

N8 has 8 MB Quad-SPI flash and no PSRAM, with integrated antenna and 40 MHz oscillator. Its module supply is 3.0–3.6 V; N8 ambient rating is −40 to85°C. It supports2.4GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth5LE sharing one antenna, not5GHzWi-Fi or BluetoothClassic. Antenna placement, enclosure clearance and final RF testing remain design requirements. Prefer antenna beyond the carrier edge; preserve the manufacturer's keepout geometry and at least15mm enclosure clearance around the antenna. Module certificates alone do not qualify this assembled CNC product. [Espressif module datasheet](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-s3-wroom-1_wroom-1u_datasheet_en.pdf), [module placement guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/pcb-layout-design.html)

U1 pin2 uses ESP_3V3; pins1/40/41 connect to LOGIC_GND. C1 is22µF/16V X7R10%, with a required effective minimum10µF; local100nF bypasses support the module and logic. Exact capacitor MPNs/bias curves are pending. The22µF plus six locally charged100nF parts have **28.658µF maximum** under the stated initial/temperature assumptions. The external adapter's100nF is powered separately and excluded.

The existing0.7A ESP output reservation includes a new5mA allowance for this support circuitry; the corresponding existing CORE5 reservation remains0.62A. At the logic regulator's analytical1.156ms fastest startup, the new capacitors add **82.832mA** atESP3V3, approximately **67.134mA** atCORE5 with85% assumed efficiency. Running plus this extra charge is0.782832A before any already-budgeted regulator output-cap charging, below the1A regulator rating under these assumptions. Integrating this block requires increasing the whole logic-output charge reservation from170mA to at least250mA and checking all existing/external capacitance. Radio peaks and module temperature require measurement; a datasheet RF current example is not a worst-case complete firmware budget.

The actual upstream reset boundary is **ESP_CHIP_PU**, a push-pull signal. U2 SN74LVC1G97 is configured as a Schmitt AND: pin1 grounded, pin3 manual-reset release, pin6 ESP_CHIP_PU, pin4 to module EN. A10k/100nF network conditions the manual input; EN has47k toground. JRESET accepts only a normally-open dry contact toground and cannot short the upstream driver. Below valid supply,47k helps define EN, but zero-supply Ioff does not prove every intermediate power ramp. Normal reset requires both local release and the upstream qualifier. [TI configurable gate, Tables1–2/Figure4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g97.pdf)

GPIO0 has10k pull-up; GPIO45/46/3 have10k pull-downs. These retain3.3V flash supply selection and enable manual download selection with GPIO0 low/GPIO46 low under default eFuse settings. For recovery, holdBOOT low, holdRESET at least20ms, releaseRESET, keepBOOT low at least3ms after EN rises, then release. Download security/eFuse settings can disable recovery and must be managed separately; no eFuse programming occurred. The board must be powered normally and the machine disarmed before service. Reset/BOOT do not constitute machine emergency stopping.

## USB service without USB power feed

GPIO19/module13 is D− and GPIO20/module14 is D+. Each has22Ω series resistance near the module. U5 **TS3USB221ARSER** connects common D+/D− (pins8/7) to port1 (pins1/2); select9 is grounded and port2 pins3/4 are NC. Its I/O specification accepts5.5V, and the zero-supply data leakage specification is±2µA. The earlier TS3USB31E choice was rejected because its normal I/O range ends atVCC. The final221A switch's6Ω maximum resistance plus22Ω resistors require USB signal-integrity and enumeration checks. [TI switch datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ts3usb221a.pdf)

U6 uses the same Schmitt gate as an inverter: pins1/5 atESP3V3, pins2/3 grounded, VBUS sense atpin6, /OE atpin4. The10k/100k divider senses USBVBUS without feeding any board power rail. With4.4–5.25V VBUS,2% resistor allowance and5µA input-leakage screening, sense is3.939–4.835V, below the gate's5.5V input limit. Zero VBUS commands disconnect; valid VBUS commands connection while local power exists. The intermediate falling-VBUS threshold and partial-power behavior are not a USB compliance certification.

Two separate5.1k CC resistors advertise a sink-facing USB2 device. A6/B6 join D+, A7/B7 join D−, SBU contacts are NC. USBVBUS never powersESP or the controller; there is noPD negotiation. D1 TPD2EUSB30DRTR protects both data lines toground; D2–D4 TPD1E10B06DPYR coverVBUS/CC1/CC2. These are sourced ESD candidates, not proof that their residual pulse voltage or this PCB survives a specified test. The provisional shell bond is0Ω toLOGIC_GND and needs enclosure/shield review; this port is not galvanically isolated. [TI data protection](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd2eusb30.pdf), [TI VBUS/CC protection](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd1e10b06.pdf)

JUSB1 represents standard Type-C USB2 electrical contacts plus one grouped shell boundary. GCTUSB4105-GF-A is a sourcing candidate; its manufacturer's downloadable drawing returnedHTTP403, so **physical solder-pad mapping, connector MPN and footprint are intentionally unassigned**. The schematic does not invent shell-pad numbers or a fabrication footprint. [GCT connector family](https://gct.co/connector/usb4105)

## Serial, firmware and reserved GPIO

JHOST is receive-only3.3V host health data at1152008N1, through1k and the ESP-powered U3 buffer toGPIO4. The same dual buffer routes a separate recovery adapter TX toUART0 RX/module36. Both external inputs have100k ground defaults so an idle unpowered external transmitter is not fed by a local pull-up. U4 buffers module37/UART0TX toward the adapter and is powered **only from the adapter's3.0–3.6V VREF**. Its output becomes high impedance at zeroVREF. VREF is not a board-power pin. Both buffer families have Ioff support; intermediate rails, cable transients, senderVOH/VOL, slew and actual UART operation still require qualification. The calculator's2.2V Schmitt threshold is an engineering screen, not a guaranteed interpolation across every supply value. [TI receive buffer](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g17.pdf), [TI transmit buffer](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g17.pdf)

Current firmware emits diagnostic JSON using USB Serial/JTAG, leaves hostRX disabled by default and has a GPIO4 compile-only variant. The physical candidate now assigns that available non-strap pin; **this does not turn the existing build into a flashed or qualified board profile**. Firmware does not configure a hostTX, actuator, step, relay or reset output. UART freshness remains advisory. USB Serial/JTAG allows download/console operation, with documented sleep and pin-reconfiguration limitations. [Espressif USB console guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/v5.5.4/esp32s3/api-guides/usb-serial-jtag-console.html)

JRES reservesGPIO5–12 as internal3.3V logic contacts. They are not industrial inputs,5V tolerant ports, field-power outputs, or implemented accessory interfaces. External circuitry must not drive them when the ESP is unpowered. Future accessory hardware/firmware must define protection, ownership and safe defaults before these become product ports.

## Verification and remaining gates

**KiCad10.0.4 ERC has zero violations;24 host tests pass.** Tests mutate power feed, USB orientation/CC/select, reset-driver contention, boot straps, module ground pad, ESD return, UART buffer supply domains, part variants, capacitor overload and invalid numbers. The gate truth table is checked independently against the manufacturer's function table. PDF overview and module/reset/USB detail were inspected. Source research found and corrected the initial USB-switch voltage-range mistake before freezing this block.

Reproduce with `python tools/hardware_build_esp_service.py`, fresh KiCad netlist/ERC/PDF exports, `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_esp_service_hardware.py -v`, then `python tools/hardware_validate_esp_service.py`. Regeneration replaces generated files. There has been no PCB layout/DRC, flashed device, USB/RF/EMC measurement, accessory implementation, pricing approval or manufacturing release.
