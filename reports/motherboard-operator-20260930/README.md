# Motherboard operator, probes and protected ports

The frozen source contains **194 real fitted parts and 579 numbered electrical pads** on the same motherboard. Native KiCad XML matches the independently transcribed pin/net contract. Native ERC reports zero errors and two unsuppressed warnings for the two intentionally floating ISO1212 substrate islands. All 194 footprints load in native pcbnew with the expected pad numbers. There are 591 total pad shapes when the TPS2662 exposed-pad tabs and paste shapes are included.

The circuit supplies twelve protected 5 V contact inputs (ten pendant selectors, MPG enable and measurement-module presence), two dedicated unfiltered isolated probes, four independently protected encoder supplies, an independently protected MPG supply, the measurement-module supply and two field-A probe supplies. None of these consume a general DI. The actual three-register input-only status chain carries the twenty inputs/fault flags and a far-end numeric 0xA marker; nearest-register H is serialized as bit 0. The required double-read agreement and fault policy remain gateware work.

The FPGA's two new slow enable requests have physical 4.7 kΩ, 1% LOW defaults. Actual Schmitt buffers restore these requests and configuration-done before a hardware AND permits the five encoder/MPG supplies. Measurement-module power additionally requires the separate configuration request and actual plug-presence signal. At a conservative 3.6 V weak-pull supply, 27 kΩ minimum unused-user-mode pull-up and 20 µA total upward leakage, the default screen is 0.619 V. The actual SN74LVC1G17 minimum falling threshold is 0.89 V at 3 V. Reset/configuration has no internal pull according to the retained FPGA primary review; the weak pull-up is an unused-user-mode condition.

TI's TPS2553-1 bounding equations, including 1% resistor tolerance and a 150 K/100 ppm allowance, give 207–284 mA for each 200 mA encoder supply, 102–147 mA for the 100 mA MPG supply and 151–212 mA for the 150 mA measurement supply. These are engineering equation screens, separate from assembled measurements. FAULT flags are not voltage readings and are only valid with the actual parent rail monitor and region qualification. No per-port voltage ADC is claimed.

The two TPS26624 probe branches are drawn with an exact 49.9 kΩ candidate resistor. **Guaranteed overload and hard-short bounds at that value remain an engineering/sourcing gate.** The published 44.2 kΩ maximum of 159 mA exceeds the frozen 150 mA limit and is not used as proof. No guaranteed limit has been invented by interpolating a typical graph. The PCB must not be released on the strength of this source alone.

The dedicated ISO1212 probe pair retains reviewed threshold/current resistors and separate SUB copper islands, with no ordinary-DI input capacitor. Bidirectional 33 V TVS parts protect the 24 V interface without intentionally shorting a negative input through a unidirectional suppressor. The twelve selector/contact inputs instead use bidirectional 5.5 V ESD devices, a series resistor, rail-clamp diodes, a contact filter and real Schmitt inputs. These are typed 5 V contacts, not 24 V sensor inputs.

A real LM4040 2.5 V shunt reference and resistor-fed terminal provide the single-ended MPG bias. At the stated minimum-rail reference screen, the 1.8 kΩ feed supplies 1.231 mA: a 1 mA external load leaves more than the 80 µA full-temperature minimum shunt current. The 22 Ω terminal resistor causes 22 mV drop at 1 mA. It is not an arbitrary sensor power supply.

Physical terminal footprints and shielding are parent work, not fitted placeholder connectors here.
