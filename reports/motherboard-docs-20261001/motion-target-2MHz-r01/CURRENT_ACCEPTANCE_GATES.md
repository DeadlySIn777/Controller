# Current controller acceptance gates

30 September 2026. This register turns already-recorded user decisions into named review gates. It does not add installed capabilities or report acceptance. Read with [project memory](../PROJECT_MEMORY.md), the [64-requirement trace](../reports/audit-2026-09-30/REQUIREMENTS_AUDIT.md), the [200 preserved proposals](FEATURE_CATALOG.md) and the [current audit](../reports/audit-2026-09-30/AUDIT_REPORT.md).

The current user-selected product is one active custom motherboard with a soldered FPGA and all twelve motor interfaces fitted, at least eight usable motors, and individual enable and alarm on every motor, four standard ABZ encoders, 24 general DI, 16 smart general DO with qualified current/fault diagnostics, probes/frequency/PWM/process control, analog I/O, RS-485/CAN, power protection and watchdog/permission hardware. The passive fixed base/thirteen-custom-card rack, removable FPGA/field cards and custom MX4/XTB motors-9–12 topology are explicitly superseded by [IS-2](MOTHERBOARD_IS2.md). Qualified purchased compute, ESP and settings/storage modules remain replaceable; whole-board repair uses retained keyed terminal/harness groups and needs a separate service proof. Screen classes 7/10/12/14/18/21 inches and further qualified sizes do not determine motion/I/O capacity. US$400 remains a preliminary assembly reference; complete kits are separately costed. Original software is commercial, with paid offline licences, customer-sourceable proven peripherals and legitimate replacement without repurchase.

**Current evidence boundary:** [MC r0.4/native revision 20260930-14](../reports/board-completion-20260930/mc-integrated-r04/review.json) is a partial 327-part motion card, with fresh ERC zero and regular DRC zero (`ignored_checks: []`), but 499 capped unconnected items and 711 independently counted missing physical joins. Its 105 native/source inputs remain unchanged. [Post-r0.4 host checks](../reports/board-completion-20260930/post-r04-host-validation/README.md) report 869 cases (864 passed/five Windows symlink skips) and 1,272 unchanged source/research hashes. These results do not accept a complete machine/controller gate. Historical TPS3431 timing failure and the repaired historical reset pin map are distinguished from the current integrated dual TPS3851 candidate below. The user has now resolved that physical conflict in favour of the one motherboard; this records explicit supersession of the rack/card/MX4 boundaries, not acceptance of missing engineering. The untouched external IS-1 logical/electrical/fault/protocol source must be reconciled through versioned IS-2 board-region identity and capability meanings.

## Hardware/software integration and ownership

The recorded working allocation is Codex for schematics, power/protection, connectors, PCB and BOM; Claude for FPGA logic, CNC1 protocol/driver and configuration-service integration. A frozen joint specification **IS-1** exists in the separate `claude/interface-spec` worktree at `C:/Users/Gluis/claude-worktrees/controller-interface-spec`, committed in `0d879b2`; `67b1bfc` adds a CNC3 codec and host/model implementation. Its master is `docs/CONTROLLER_INTERFACE_SPEC.md` and its six machine-readable records live under `spec/`. It binds channel/port identities, enable/alarm polarities, framing, card identity, power/reset/watchdog behavior, telemetry validity/age, timing and recovery. It is not integrated into the main checkout or implemented as complete RTL/HAL/hardware. Preserve that frozen revision unchanged as source history; IS-2 supersedes its passive-base/card/MX4 physical allocation and requires reviewed integration of retained logical/electrical/fault contracts; do not silently edit it or treat the older 128-byte expansion draft as its actual 176-byte feedback/96-byte command ABI.

An actual Git baseline exists: `e994311` (28 September 2026). Later commits `80cc958`, `c61ccbf` and `477c225` record product/commercial decisions. A separate committed worktree therefore exists; controlled integration into main and agreement that both drawn hardware and executable software meet the retained IS-1 contracts plus versioned IS-2 physical/identity transition remain open. Preserve the baseline and user changes; interface changes require explicit versioning and independently reviewed merge evidence. This audit creates no production firmware, bitstream or physical interface approval. IS-1's quantity-100 cost range is an unquoted estimate of US$955–1,500 for the eight-motor/four-encoder controller before the screen; its proposed selling price is US$1,739–2,729. Those rack/card figures are a historical unquoted estimate, not the current motherboard BOM or an adopted selling price. Complete current circuit, manufacture, service and package costs remain open; physical consolidation alone does not prove profitability.
