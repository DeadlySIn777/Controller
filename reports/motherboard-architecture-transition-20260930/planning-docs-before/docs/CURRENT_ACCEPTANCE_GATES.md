# Current controller acceptance gates

30 September 2026. This register turns already-recorded user decisions into named review gates. It does not add installed capabilities or report acceptance. Read with [project memory](../PROJECT_MEMORY.md), the [64-requirement trace](../reports/audit-2026-09-30/REQUIREMENTS_AUDIT.md), the [200 preserved proposals](FEATURE_CATALOG.md) and the [current audit](../reports/audit-2026-09-30/AUDIT_REPORT.md).

The current product is eight standard motor channels plus motors 9–12 on a plug-in module, optionally factory-fitted; a fixed wiring base with a replaceable FPGA motion card; individual enable/alarm per motor; 24 general inputs and 16 general outputs. Screen classes 7/10/12/14/18/21 inches and further qualified sizes do not determine motion/I/O capacity. US$400 is a preliminary controller-assembly reference. Complete kits are separately costed. Original application software is commercial; paid offline licences, ordinary customer-sourceable peripherals and legitimate module replacement without repurchase remain required.

**Current evidence boundary:** [MC r0.4/native revision 20260930-14](../reports/board-completion-20260930/mc-integrated-r04/review.json) is a partial 327-part motion card, with fresh ERC zero and regular DRC zero (`ignored_checks: []`), but 499 capped unconnected items and 711 independently counted missing physical joins. Its 105 native/source inputs remain unchanged. [Post-r0.4 host checks](../reports/board-completion-20260930/post-r04-host-validation/README.md) report 869 cases (864 passed/five Windows symlink skips) and 1,272 unchanged source/research hashes. These results do not accept a complete machine/controller gate. Historical TPS3431 timing failure and the repaired historical reset pin map are distinguished from the current integrated dual TPS3851 candidate below. The newer one-custom-PCB direction remains unresolved against frozen IS-1's custom-card and service/expansion topology; the compute-bay decision does not waive those requirements.

## Hardware/software integration and ownership

The recorded working allocation is Codex for schematics, power/protection, connectors, PCB and BOM; Claude for FPGA logic, CNC1 protocol/driver and configuration-service integration. A frozen joint specification **IS-1** exists in the separate `claude/interface-spec` worktree at `C:/Users/Gluis/claude-worktrees/controller-interface-spec`, committed in `0d879b2`; `67b1bfc` adds a CNC3 codec and host/model implementation. Its master is `docs/CONTROLLER_INTERFACE_SPEC.md` and its six machine-readable records live under `spec/`. It binds channel/port identities, enable/alarm polarities, framing, card identity, power/reset/watchdog behavior, telemetry validity/age, timing and recovery. It is not integrated into the main checkout or implemented as complete RTL/HAL/hardware. Preserve that frozen revision and review integration against it; do not silently edit it or treat the older 128-byte expansion draft as its actual 176-byte feedback/96-byte command ABI.

An actual Git baseline exists: `e994311` (28 September 2026). Later commits `80cc958`, `c61ccbf` and `477c225` record product/commercial decisions. A separate committed worktree therefore exists; controlled integration into main and agreement that both drawn hardware and executable software meet IS-1 remain open. Preserve the baseline and user changes; interface changes require explicit versioning and independently reviewed merge evidence. This audit creates no production firmware, bitstream or physical interface approval. IS-1's quantity-100 cost range is an unquoted estimate of US$955–1,500 for the eight-motor/four-encoder controller before the screen; its proposed selling price is US$1,739–2,729. That proposal exceeds the US$400 reference and requires a concrete cost/architecture review before adoption as a manufacturable product.
