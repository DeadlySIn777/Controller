# Primary-source pin, lands and retained electrical review

30 September 2026. The selected part remains **TI ISO1212DBQR**, DBQ 16-pin SSOP. The current official [ISO1211/ISO1212 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/iso1212.pdf) was checked; the retained source is `research/digital-inputs/iso1212.pdf`, SHA-256 `41e0bc7308dd4e0cc8c7f30efb6a2cd87bcc865d9195095ff849be6036cb2328`, copied into the frozen review. Its Rev. G header and 2026 history retain the earlier documented date mismatch; actual source bytes govern.

The pin table and top-view Figure 4-2 were visually checked in `research/digital-inputs/pins.png`: 1/8 GND1, 2 VCC1, 3 EN, 4 OUT1, 5 OUT2, 6/7 NC, 9 FGND2, 10 IN2, 11 SENSE2, 12 SUB2, 13 SUB1, 14 FGND1, 15 IN1, 16 SENSE1. Both logic grounds are connected, EN is tied to IO_3V3, NC6/7 have native no-connect declarations and each SUB gets its own net. Separate field/logic grounds and a common field return do not establish channel-to-channel galvanic isolation or a full-board rating.

The [DBQ0016A outline](ti-dbq-package-outline.png), [example board layout](ti-dbq-land-example.png) and [stencil example](ti-dbq-stencil-example.png) come from zero-based PDF pages 43/44/45, drawing **4214846/A, 03/2014**. The motherboard footprint directly uses the example's 16 rectangular 1.60 × 0.41 mm metal lands, 0.635 mm pitch and 5.40 mm opposing centre separation. Pin 1 is upper-left; pins 1–8 descend left, 9–16 ascend right. Maximum molded body is 3.98 × 5.00 mm and maximum package height 1.75 mm. The footprint courtyard is a project allowance 0.25 mm outside max body/copper, not a manufacturer clearance or a final assembly rule. Board SUB islands and equipment isolation spacing remain separate layout work.

The older generic KiCad/NXP SOT519-1 footprint uses 1.65 × 0.40 mm roundrect pads on 5.25 mm centre separation. It is left unchanged in the historical baseline. The new `CNC_MB_DI:ISO1212_DBQ16_TI` is separately generated and [native-loaded](native-footprint-review.json), with exact pad numbers/coordinates/dimensions/shapes/layers. Its 3D STEP is a nominal SSOP visual body from KiCad and is not a guaranteed TI mechanical clearance model. Final mask/paste/stencil/tolerance/process acceptance still requires the parent manufacturing review; no stencil export or assembly qualification is claimed.

The 1 kΩ SMM02040C1001FB300 threshold/pulse resistor, 562 Ω CRCW0603562RFKEA sense resistor and 06033C104JAT2A 100 nF logic bypass retain TI ISO1212EVM references. The 100 kΩ CRCW0603100KFKEA output bias and 10 nF/100 V C0G C2012C0G2A103J125AA filter retain the reviewed baseline choices. Primary sources and byte hashes are in `research/digital-inputs/sources.json`, preserved in this review. These are source-backed candidates, not supplier availability, physical latency or component-substitution approval.

Frozen sensor allocation is checked from archived IS-1 section 03 XDI0/XDI1/XDI2 contacts 9/10 and section 04 §10.2: 0.20 A shared continuous sensor supply per group, three groups/0.60 A. The section 03 “On with FIELD_A” wording contradicts its FIELD_B entry/net budget; this new sheet claims no fitted source or power-up sequencing. Keep the actual parent supply/default contract coordinated and test it. The source's 2.47 mA headline must not replace TI's 2.75 mA guaranteed-table maximum in calculations. No sensor supply limiter, measurement, controller, firmware-mode selection or input-valid logic is implemented by the passive frontends.
