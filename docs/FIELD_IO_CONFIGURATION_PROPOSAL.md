# Configurable field I/O — versioned proposal

**Reviewed 18 October 2026.** [Documentation index](README.md) · [Current engineering status](../reports/ENGINEERING_STATUS.md). FIO-P1 remains a versioned proposal. Current source representation, disconnected I/O simulation and diagnostic target compilation do not establish configurable voltage/polarity/current hardware or physical ratings.

30 September 2026. **FIO-P1, proposed IS-2 work; not implemented or electrically qualified.** Records the user's request for 24 DI / 16 DO, configurable 12/24 V field operation, PNP/NPN compatibility, 0.5/1/2 A output tiers, a customer-supplied AUX12 rail, physical bank fuses and per-channel electronic protection. It does not change frozen IS-1, current hardware, machine profiles, CLI limits or the public schema. The one-custom-motherboard/service-contract reconciliation remains separate.

The proposal is useful if configuration describes **real fitted hardware and wiring**. A setting cannot turn the selected high-side switch into a low-side switch, manufacture a 12 V supply, increase a connector's rating or independently set every channel when the IC shares a setting across a pair.

## Preserved scope and current boundary

Retain all twelve complete motor interfaces fitted on the one active motherboard, at least eight usable motors and individual enables/alarms on every channel, four base ABZ encoders, 24 general digital inputs, 16 smart general digital outputs, the remaining required interfaces, hardware fault inhibition and offline setup/recovery. This supersedes the earlier eight-plus-custom-expansion topology. Field I/O is independent of display size, compute tier and licence tier; no fitted motor unlock is required. Encoder expansion remains a separate unqualified target.

Frozen IS-1 specifies sourcing DO with **0.5 A continuous/channel, 2 A/four-output group**, two separately protected field entries and full output diagnostics. Its selected exact output driver is **TPS274C65ASRHAR**. These remain the active limits; this document does not label the unfinished DO banks implemented. Higher current, different field voltage or sinking DO needs a versioned electrical, protocol, firmware and qualification change. ## Configurable options and their hardware consequences

| Requested option | Proposed behavior | What must exist before it can be offered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PNP sensor | A sinking DI front end accepts a sensor that sources current | Qualified threshold/current/filter/protection circuit and declared sensor common |
| NPN sensor | A sourcing DI front end provides current to a sensor that sinks it | A real selectable wiring/front-end path; software inversion alone cannot change the current path |
| High-side / sourcing DO | Output connects its field-bank positive supply to the load | Selected TPS274C65AS circuit, field-side power and isolation, protected supply, complete diagnostics |
| Low-side / sinking DO | Output connects a load return to its field common | Separately selected protected low-side hardware with its own diagnostics and inhibit proof, or a qualified third-party interface; TPS274C65AS cannot provide this mode |
| 12 V / 24 V field banks | Outputs switch the actual externally supplied bank voltage | A rated power path and explicit operating range, sensing, undervoltage/transient behavior and a qualified external source. Shared VS means channels on one chip share the physical voltage. Per-point mixed supplies require additional hardware. |
| Customer AUX12 | Protected distribution of a declared external 12 V source | Separate labelled entry, fuse/current limit, reverse/transient/back-feed control, monitored rail and declared return/isolation. No AUX12 voltage is generated by a configuration setting. Never connect it directly to AUX24. |
| 0.5 A tier | Retain the frozen channel/group limits | The existing proposal's default 0x6 current-limit setting and completed bank protection/thermal/diagnostic qualification |
| 1 A tier | Candidate higher load rating | Re-rate field entries, each four-channel group, terminals, copper, cooling, load transients and firmware; select a guaranteed current-limit range with headroom. No immediate profile increase. |
| 2 A tier | Candidate higher load rating | The selected AS part cannot guarantee this tier. Evaluate **TPS274C65ASHRHAR** or another exact part as a separate candidate, retaining diagnostics; prove simultaneous-bank current, thermal behavior and protection before selection. |
| DIP switch or guided profile | Select a supported fitted/wired profile with power off | Readback of the real selector and hardware identity, validation against capability metadata, default-off response to an invalid setting, generated wiring guidance and deliberate commissioning. No selector circuit is drawn here. |

TI documents both sourcing and sinking ISO1212 input arrangements, but they use different physical connections. Its bidirectional example uses both receiver channels for one field point; adopting that example unchanged for 24 points would require **24 ISO1212 packages**, rather than the 12 packages of a unidirectional DI24 bank. A bridge or selectable-common alternative needs its own diode-drop, threshold, isolation, surge and cost review. The existing historical 22-input circuit is not a configurable DI24 implementation. [TI ISO1212, sections 8.2.1–8.2.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/iso1212.pdf)

## Current setting is not a load rating

The intended UI tiers describe permitted **continuous load**, together with a separate permitted peak/duration. Hardware current-limit codes describe a tolerance range during overload. The source review records the actual codes and min/max limits; neither the UI nor a DIP legend should promise an exact 0.500/1.000/2.000 A electronic trip.

The selected driver has shared current-limit resources for CH1/2 and CH3/4. The frozen IOL1 configuration describes per-output codes; arbitrary different codes for two paired outputs are not realizable. All-default 0x6 remains realizable. IS-2 must either expose the paired resource explicitly or reject incompatible per-output requests before preview/apply; it must not silently choose the higher neighbour setting. This needs coordination with the logic/service owner before any contract change. [TI registers DEV_CONFIG1–3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps274c65.pdf)

Higher current also changes the whole bank. Sixteen 0.5/1/2 A loads respectively draw **8/16/32 A** before auxiliary loads; at 24 V their external load powers are **192/384/768 W**. A four-output group would draw **2/4/8 A**. These are arithmetic proposals, not rated motherboard capacities. The frozen 2 A group and field entries must not be reused as evidence for the larger tiers.

## Protection and fault policy

Use coordinated physical bank/entry fuses and the selected channels' electronic protection. A bank fuse protects its feed/conductors under its specified time-current and interrupt rating; it is not an accurate per-output current limiter. Add electronic branch limits only where they close a real power/containment requirement. Account for short-circuit overshoot, inductive release, cable energy and all simultaneous loads rather than assuming the programmed current limit is instantaneous.

Retain independent hardware DO_EN inhibition, pull-down defaults, OUT_EN latch/rearm, MCU/link watchdogs, accepted-frame CRC/freshness and readback. Hold outputs disabled through initialization and register verification; driver reset defaults are not the controller's accepted profile. Fault clearing, rail recovery or changing a switch must never restart an output automatically.

The future configuration service can expose qualified limits, debounce, expected diagnostic load, approved inrush and named fault responses. It must not expose unrestricted writes that disable required current/thermal protection, bypass hardware permits, enable automatic retry or mask stopping faults. A qualified 12 V profile may distinguish a chip's fixed 24 V-oriented warning from the actual rail assessment, while retaining the raw warning, validity/age and independent undervoltage protection. No warning is globally suppressed to make an unqualified voltage mode appear healthy.

Current, open-load, temperature and supply reports must carry validity and age. An OFF, reverse-blocked, unpowered or stale path cannot be presented as a measured zero-current healthy load. Treat low-current and inductive-load diagnoses as tested profile behavior, with their uncertainty shown. Software licensing and AI must never block stopping or necessary diagnostics/recovery.

## Evidence and implementation sequence

First agree a versioned matrix of voltage/polarity/current options, granularity, fitted hardware and power-off wiring/selector procedure. Resolve the shared-pair ILIM and exact AS/ASH difference with the logic owner. Complete exact input/output, fuse/eFuse, rail/protection, isolation and terminal circuits with a full fitted BOM; price 1/10/100 complete controllers separately from 1/10/100 switch ICs. Then perform source/ERC checks, full PCB routing/DRC, disconnected firmware/schema tests, physical calibration and loaded fault/thermal/surge tests on the same defined variant.

The user's roughly $4/switch at 100 ICs and $40–80 whole-bank figures remain **unverified cost scenarios**. Four quad switches would be $16 at that assumed unit price, covering only DO switch ICs. They exclude DI receivers, isolation/MCUs, fuses, power/protection, terminals, passives, PCB/assembly/programming/testing and any extra sinking-output hardware. No complete bank or motherboard price has been established.
