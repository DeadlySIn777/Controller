OEA r0.2 - First-article qualification and factory acceptance test
6 October 2026 | Proposed procedure for quotation and agreement

Test the adapter and its finished harness together. Use the exact released
revision and record serial number, component substitutions, firmware/profile,
instruments, supply settings and measured results. Do not connect a moving
machine during these tests. Driver/motor tests use an isolated bench fixture.

FIRST ARTICLE - COMPLETE BEFORE RELEASING THE REMAINING QUANTITY

1. Inspect and check fit with power disconnected
Verify all 50 components, polarities, solder joints and header orientation
against the BOM and assembly drawing. J1/J2 posts point into the host;
the VM position has no pin or plated hole. Verify switch pad 2 connects to
pad 1 in the labelled 5V position and to pad 3 in EXT. Confirm break-before-
make action with an appropriate fixture; never test by hot switching.

Fit the 15.24 x 38.10 mm assembly on the named host model/revision, with
adjacent slots occupied. Check the 17.78 mm DIR-end extension, bottom SMT,
solder-tail clearance and access to J3, J4 and SW1. Check the populated
underside cannot touch host copper/components. Support the adapter while
operating the terminal or switch. Record dimensions and photographs.

2. Verify the finished harness before any power test
Check cavity numbering rather than wire colors:
  J3.1 -> PUL+, DIR+, ENA+ through the factory positive fanout
  J3.2 -> PUL-     J3.3 -> DIR-     J3.4 -> ENA-
Test continuity of every intended connection and absence of every unintended
short. Verify six separate finished driver terminations, durable labels,
length, splice insulation and retention. Approve crimp/retention and harness
drawing details before duplicating the first article.

3. Initial electrical checks
With the host and driver disconnected, check no short between EXT+ and GND,
COM+ and GND, or adjacent connector contacts. Check polarity and diode paths
against the schematic; capacitor charging must not be mistaken for a short.
Verify no adapter power rail is tied to HOST_VIO or the omitted VM position.

Use a current-limited regulated bench supply at J4 and a logic source with
shared GND. Start with STEP, DIR and EN low. Use an initial 100 mA current
limit for the unloaded unit; resolve unexpected current or heating before
raising a limit or attaching loads. Record V_LOGIC, V5_REG, COM+ and input
current. V_LOGIC is nominally 3.343 V; a proposed steady-state acceptance band
is 3.20-3.50 V after settling. Any failure requires engineering review.

4. Run the normal supply/selector matrix
Power off both sources and discharge before each selector change.

Terminal supply    Selector   Required observation
5.00 V             EXT        COM+ follows external supply; logic stays powered.
12.00 V            5V         Buck supplies nominal 5 V; logic stays powered.
24.00 V            5V         Buck supplies nominal 5 V; logic stays powered.
24.00 V            EXT        COM+ follows external supply; logic stays powered.

For each case, test unloaded and with 20 mA per signal channel, including
all three channels active together (60 mA total driver-input load). A fixture
must emulate driver inputs correctly: each load goes between COM+ and its
MINUS output. Confirm a HIGH controller input turns the corresponding sink
ON, a LOW turns it OFF, and the other two channels remain independent.

Repeat with both 3.3 V and 5 V host logic. Record loaded COM+ at the far end
of the supplied harness, ON voltage at each MINUS terminal, OFF leakage and
actual current. Proposed sink acceptance is <=0.20 V at 20 mA at room
temperature; establish the full ambient/load limits on the first article.
The exact drive must receive its required input current and voltage after
all diode, wire and sink losses. Do not accept '5V' silkscreen or an unloaded
5 V reading as proof of a compatible driver input.

Qualify supply tolerance and declared ambient limits separately. In 5 V
EXT mode, test the minimum intended source voltage with the actual harness.
In buck mode, verify start/restart at the minimum intended 12 V source and
regulation at the maximum intended 24 V source. The initial terminal target
is 4.75-26.4 V; that range is not released until these measurements pass.
Do not rate 5 V terminal input with the selector in 5V mode as supported.

5. Driver timing, enable and sequencing
Use the exact drive model, revision and its approved 5 V/24 V input setting.
Measure PUL/DIR/ENA at the driver's terminals with the finished cable. Verify
input currents, pulse high/low time, DIR setup/hold and EN lead time against
that manual. Sweep the intended operating rate, then exercise the declared
maximum under load and confirm commanded counts/direction without missing
or extra steps. Do not copy a driver's maximum rate onto the adapter without
measuring the complete interface.

Initialize STEP low before output-enable release. Test adapter-first,
host-first and simultaneous power-up; host reset; power-down; brownout;
and loss/reconnection of each supply and signal cable. Record supervisor
release and any pulse edge or EN transition. An already-HIGH STEP can produce
an edge when the buffer becomes active. An open/unpowered ENA input may
enable the drive. Approve the actual startup/disable sequence explicitly;
the adapter is not an emergency stop or independent machine inhibit.

6. Power/protection and sustained-load qualification
Check rapid input removal/collapse and stored-charge discharge, including
the isolated buck input reservoir and the LDO output. Verify no damaging
reverse current, rail overshoot or backfeed into the host. With suitable
engineering equipment, qualify reverse-polarity behavior, input ringing,
fuse clearing/fault energy and the intended supply-lead arrangement.
Do not conduct destructive fault tests on the customer's controller/drive.
Measure steady temperatures at worst declared source, ambient and load.
Record the actual supported limits; component datasheets alone are not an
assembled-board surge, thermal or safety certification.

FACTORY TEST - EVERY DELIVERED ADAPTER AND HARNESS

After the first article establishes acceptance limits, PCBWay shall:
  1. Inspect assembly, polarity, header orientation and connector/switch labels.
  2. Perform the full harness continuity/short, retention and label checks.
  3. Check unpowered shorts and both selector continuity states.
  4. Run all four normal supply/selector cases above with the agreed fixture.
  5. Verify all three channels at 3.3 V and 5 V logic, unloaded and at the
     agreed load, recording supply current, COM+ and sink voltage.
  6. Run the agreed pulse/direction/enable pattern at the qualified rate.
  7. Recheck after final connector-tail trimming and depaneling; attach the
     unit test record and package the matched board/harness for delivery.

Use the first article's written limits and fixture revision for pass/fail.
Unspecified or failed results require disposition; do not substitute an ERC,
DRC, visual inspection or generic power-on test for these functional checks.
Quote one-time qualification/fixture work separately from recurring unit tests.
