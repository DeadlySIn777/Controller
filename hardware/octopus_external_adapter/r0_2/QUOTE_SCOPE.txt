REVISED FUNCTION
The adapter accepts reviewed 3.3 V or 5 V STEP/DIR/ENABLE controller inputs.
A separate EXT+/GND screw terminal powers it in both switch positions.
The non-shorting 5V / EXT selector selects driver COM+ only:
  - 12 or 24 V at the terminal, selector 5V: onboard nominal 5 V control
    supply, less the output diode and wiring losses.
  - Regulated 5 V at the terminal, selector EXT: external 5 V control supply.
  - Regulated 24 V at the terminal, selector EXT: external 24 V control supply.
  - 12 V with EXT is allowed only for an individually approved driver profile.
The design targets common-positive STEP/DIR/ENABLE inputs. It does not claim
all BTT/SKR boards, all external drivers, differential signaling or isolation.
The socket motor-voltage contact and plated hole are omitted.
