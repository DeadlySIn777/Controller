OEA r0.2 logic, selector and connector review
2026-10-06. Engineering source candidate; not a manufacturing release.

Decision and limits
The adapter accepts host STEP/DIR/EN commands nominally 3.3 V or 5 V, powered
independently from a protected external supply. Its output topology is three
common-positive, nonisolated, open-drain current sinks. It is not a differential
line driver and cannot represent all external motor-driver input types.

logic_spec.json is the exact part/pad/net authority for the 30 parts owned here.
Power parts are separately defined in power_spec.json by the power-design agent.
Shared names: V_LOGIC = local nominal 3.3 V-class logic rail (3.3428 V nominal
with the selected LT3014B divider, not a precision 3.300 V rail); V5_REG = buck output;
V_EXT_PROT = protected external input; EXT_RAW = screw-terminal positive;
COM_PLUS = switched cable common; GND is permanently shared.

External input 5 V, switch EXT: drive common follows protected external 5 V.
External input 12/24 V, switch 5V: drive common uses the 5 V buck through D1.
External input 12/24 V, switch EXT: drive common follows protected external rail.
External 5 V is NOT a qualified input for the buck's regulated-5 V mode.
The input must be a regulated DC source within the separately declared power
limits. No 48 V signal-reference or universal-driver claim is authorized.

Logic and startup
U1 SN74LVC2G126DCUR controls STEP and DIR. U2 SN74LVC1G126DCKR controls EN.
Both use V_LOGIC and accept input voltages up to 5.5 V, including when VCC is zero;
their specified Ioff function supports partial-power-down operation.
They are standard CMOS buffers, not Schmitt triggers. Host edge quality must
meet the selected IC's input-transition specification. Their short local paths
must not be treated as long unconditioned field-signal inputs.

Each host input has 220 ohm series resistance. STEP and DIR have 100k pulldowns.
EN has a 10k pullup to HOST_VIO and a 100k pulldown. HOST_VIO is only the host's
3.3/5 V logic reference: it never supplies the buck, LDO, output optocouplers or
local-rail pullups. This avoids an intentional external-rail backfeed into an
unpowered host. Input leakage and actual off-power host behavior still require
qualification; no literal zero-current guarantee is implied.

U3 TPS3839G33DBZR monitors V_LOGIC, using a nominal 3.08 V threshold and a
200 ms typical reset-release delay. Its push-pull RESET_N drives LOGIC_READY,
which enables all three active-high OE pins. R14 holds OE low if undriven.
The supervisor has a specified valid reset for VDD above 0.6 V. The falling
threshold is3.003-3.126 V over its specified temperature range; hysteresis31 mV
is typical. Reset release delay is120-350 ms. Falling response20 us is typical,
not a maximum bound for an arbitrarily fast brownout. Under specified loads,
RESET low is<=0.4 V and high is>=VDD-0.4 V, suitable for the OE logic thresholds.
These values do not constitute measured assembly startup behavior. It is independent
of the regulator's PG pin, whose valid range alone was not adequate evidence
for the intended brownout behavior. All three ICs have local 100nF bypassing.

Each buffer drives a MOSFET gate through 220 ohms. Each gate has a 10k pulldown.
The latter limits a specified 10 uA off-state buffer leakage contribution to
approximately 0.1 V rather than relying on a weak floating MOSFET gate.
NX6008NBKW is 60 V-rated; its actual G/S/D mapping is 1/2/3. Sources go to GND,
drains go directly to PUL_MINUS, DIR_MINUS and ENA_MINUS respectively.
Proposed load limit is 20 mA/channel, 60 mA aggregate, pending actual validation.

Logic high turns the associated sink on (asserts optocoupler current); logic low
turns it off. Host signal polarity, especially EN, must match the driver profile.
This hardware establishes a power-qualification mechanism; it does not prove
zero false pulses during every power sequence. If STEP is held high when OE is
released, the sink asserts. Firmware must initialize STEP low before enable.
Power-order, slow ramps, brownout, cable connect/disconnect and host reset require
oscilloscope testing with the actual driver load.

Drive-disabled state is distinct from sinks-off. A CL57T whose ENA input is open
may remain enabled. Host-powered EN pullup can establish one reset default, but
host-power loss cannot guarantee motor disable. This is not an emergency stop,
STO, watchdog or safety controller. Do not replace an independent inhibit chain.

Selector and reverse flow
SW1 is E-Switch EG1218, manufacturer drawing P040040 revision C. It specifies
SPDT, non-shorting timing and 0.2 A at 30 VDC. Pad 2 is common; pad 1 selects
V5_SELECT and pad 3 selects V_EXT_PROT. Ground is never switched.
The manufacturer drawing shows the actuator toward the pad-1 end and a separate
schematic common connected to pad1, but does not explicitly cross-reference
mechanical and electrical states. Actuator direction is therefore UNCONFIRMED.
Terminals are
reference-numbered and the package is mechanically symmetric. The assembly
fixture must therefore verify both actual slide positions against the finished
5V/EXT silkscreen; do not infer final left/right labels from a rotated 3D view.

D1 PMEG6010CEH,115 has anode pad 2 on V5_REG and cathode pad 1 on V5_SELECT.
It blocks the transient return path from a previously charged 24 V COM node into
the 5 V buck when the selector changes. Break-before-make alone would not do so.
No large local capacitor is fitted to COM_PLUS; R15 provides a 10k discharge
load. Switch only with BOTH controller and external supplies off and discharged.
This is not a hot-switching feature; the selected voltage must also match the
external driver's own voltage selector or resistor arrangement.

D1 reduces the regulated common voltage. At 25 C the manufacturer's pulsed
100 mA test specifies 0.4 V maximum forward drop, but this is not a complete
temperature/load qualification for this assembly. Treat 5V as a nominal signal
mode; calculate and measure the delivered voltage, MOSFET drop and resulting
input current. Combining a 4.9 V buck lower limit with a 0.4 V diode drop
leaves 4.5 V before sink and cable losses at the stated diode test conditions;
this does not guarantee >=4.5 V at the driver over temperature. Direct regulated
5 V in EXT mode avoids D1. CL57T V4.1 specifies 3.5-5 V or 24 V pulse/direction inputs and
7-16 mA logic input current. This does not establish compatibility for every
nominal-5 V driver. Its own S3 selector must match the applied signal voltage.
12 V signalling is not automatically qualified for CL57T merely because EXT
can pass 12 V.

R15 dissipation at the proposed maximum 26.4 V steady common is 69.7 mW. The
selected 0805 0.125 W resistor needs actual board-temperature derating reviewed.
SW1's 0.2 A contact rating exceeds the proposed 60 mA aggregate signal load.
It does not replace short-circuit protection or protect the sink channels from
incorrect direct connections to a power supply.

Connectors and mechanical drawing evidence
J1 is Wurth 61300811121, underside 1x8 at 2.54 mm. Pin1 EN; pin7 STEP; pin8 DIR;
pins2-6 NC. J2 is Wurth 61300711121, underside 1x7, beginning one pitch below the
VM position. Pin1 GND, pins2-5 NC, pin6 HOST_VIO, pin7 GND. There is no VM metal,
pad or hole. These are the existing r0.1 header footprints reused without change.

J3 is JST B4B-XH-A(LF)(SN), whose keyed shroud accepts XHP-4 female cable housing
with SXH-001T-P0.6 contacts. Pin1 COM_PLUS;2 PUL_MINUS;3 DIR_MINUS;4 ENA_MINUS.
The PCB part has male contacts inside a keyed shroud; the mating cable contacts
are female. The factory harness splits COM+ to three driver-positive terminals.

J4 is Phoenix Contact 1725656, MPT0.5/2-2.54. Pin1 EXT_RAW;pin2 GND. Manufacturer
body dimensions are 5.54 x 6.2 mm, height8.5 mm; electrical pins3.5 mm long,
0.5 x0.9 mm, requiring1.1 mm holes. The stock KiCad footprint also includes the
two1.1 mm nonplated locating holes at2.54 mm offset; preserve these.
The selected footprint is now Adapter:Phoenix_1725656_MPT_0p5_2_2p54_SilkClearance,
an attributed KiCad library derivative. Its sole geometric edit moves the upper
horizontal outline segment from F.SilkS to Dwgs.User so terminal polarity labels
remain legible. Its pad, drill, Fab, courtyard and model definitions are unchanged.
See j4-footprint-derivative-review.json and Adapter.pretty/KICAD-J4-ATTRIBUTION.txt.

SW1 custom footprint transcribes the manufacturer's three0.90 mm holes at2.50
mm pitch. Pad centers are(-2.5,0),(0,0),(2.5,0). The1.8 mm copper pads provide
0.45 mm nominal annuli. Body11.6 x4.0 mm, bodyheight5.4 mm, actuator2.0 mm above
body; untrimmed pins extend4.7 mm below body. On a1.6 mm PCB they can extend
about3.1 mm below it. The fabrication assembly specification must address these
tails and actual clearance to the host board. Footprint F.CrtYd includes body
tolerance and assembly clearance, not just the nominal body outline.

The15.24 mm board width is plausible, but header solder tails constrain usable
top space. A DIR-end length extension around30 mm total board length is the
current placement candidate, not universal host-fit evidence. Keep the switch
beyond the populated header rows and verify access to the selector and terminal.
Low-height logic/60 V MOSFETs may be placed on the underside within the rows;
their actual maximum heights, component overlaps, insertion depth and required
clearance need mechanical review. Never erase courtyard collisions to claim fit.

Independent LDO review
The initial TPS7A1633 candidate was replaced because its OUT<=IN+0.3 V
absolute-maximum constraint needed an independently verified reverse-discharge
solution. LT3014BES5#TRMPBF explicitly provides reverse-output protection,
including IN grounded while OUT is held above ground. It requires no external
reverse-output diode. Its ThinSOT-23-5 pad mapping is 1 IN, 2 GND, 3 NC,
4 ADJ, 5 OUT. The power specification owns this part and its passives.

The selected 174k/100k divider uses 0.1% resistors: nominal output is 3.3428 V.
Combining the E-grade full-temperature 1.18-1.26 V ADJ-reference limits with
resistor tolerance gives approximately 3.229-3.457 V before ADJ bias current.
ADJ bias is 4 nA typical, 10 nA maximum at 25 C; 10 nA adds 1.74 mV through
174k. No full-temperature maximum is asserted from that 25 C table entry.
The 22k bleeder alone draws at least 145 uA at the calculated low corner,
meeting the >=100 uA load condition of the reference accuracy specification.
The low output corner has about 72 mV margin above the supervisor's maximum
negative-going threshold plus typical hysteresis. Hysteresis is not assigned
an invented maximum; startup and ramp qualification remain required.

Output capacitance must stay >=0.47 uF effective with ESR<=3 ohms. Input
bypass guidance allows 0.1-10 uF; a nominal 220 nF/100 V part is suitable
only if voltage, temperature and tolerance leave at least 0.1 uF effective.
At a conservative 10 mA logic load and 26.4 V input, LDO dissipation is about
0.243 W when including the 450 uA worst listed ground current at that load.
The reference 150 C/W thermal metric implies a 36.5 C rise, not a thermal
measurement of this small assembled board. Typical expected logic current is
lower; actual worst-case gate activity and board temperature must be checked.

Verification boundaries
This review records datasheet inspection, pad/net specification and dimensions.
Native schematic ERC, board DRC, independent net correspondence, regulator
layout review and exact assembled-board mechanical checks must be reported by
their respective runs. None are substitutes for physical electrical testing.
The reference PDF/render stored locally is a source-inspection aid; link the
manufacturer's document rather than treating it as original project artwork.
