OEA r0.2 TPSM365R6 footprint independent source review
2026-10-06. This is source inspection, not fabrication or physical proof.

Inspected the official TPSM365R6 Rev C PDF, manufacturer drawing 4226623/F
09/2025, pages 51 and 52, as rendered images independently of the footprint
author's generator. Compared the resulting KiCad land geometry and net map.
Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpsm365r6.pdf

No pin-map or dimensional error was found in
Adapter.pretty/TI_RDN0011A_TPSM365R6.kicad_mod at review time.
Pin 4 is VOUT, pin 5 is SW. Their rectangular copper lands with comb-shaped
mask openings correctly implement the manufacturer's solder-mask-defined
pattern. The comb need not be cut out of the underlying copper rectangle.
Pin 10 is GND. Pin 11 is MODE/SYNC for the fixed-5 V variant; grounding it
selects automatic PFM/PWM. It is not an AGND pin. Pin 9 is BIAS.

Measured generated geometry:
- Eleven separate mask openings and 23 separate paste apertures.
- Pads 4/5 each have four 0.40 x 0.25 mm outer finger paste apertures, and
  three 0.90 mm wide inner windows with heights 0.515, 0.620, 0.515 mm.
- Pin 10's exposed top land and paste opening are 0.30 x 1.55 mm.
- Pins 9 and 11 have 0.25 x 0.80 mm mask/paste openings.
- Pad 1/8 mask and paste openings are 1.125 x 0.375 mm.
- Pad 2/3/6/7 mask and paste openings are 1.10 x 0.25 mm.
- Copper extends at least 0.05 mm beyond the corresponding mask boundary.
- Minimum nominal copper gap from pad 4/5 to the GND stem is 0.120 mm.
  Top pin 9/11 to pin 10 has 0.125 mm nominal gap. A local 0.10 mm copper
  clearance is consistent with this manufacturer's fine-pitch land pattern.

The source stencil example uses 0.125 mm thickness. The custom footprint
uses explicit F.Mask/F.Paste graphics over copper-only numbered pads; these
layers must be included in Gerber/stencil export and visually checked. Do not
mistake the lack of automatic per-pad paste for a paste-free component.
No under-pad vias are present; optional manufacturer via guidance therefore
does not require filled vias for this footprint as currently drawn.

This review does not establish actual PCB fabrication tolerances, solder-mask
registration, stencil fabrication acceptance, reflow yield, thermal behavior,
layout quality or electrical operation. Board-level DRC and exported layer
inspection remain distinct checks.
