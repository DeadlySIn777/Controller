OEA r0.2 — prototype assembly and wiring requirements
Engineering candidate. Quote and DFM review only; no production release.

DELIVERY
Quote five completely assembled adapters and five matching finished signal
harnesses (four axes and one spare). PCBWay supplies all PCB components,
connectors, wire, crimps, splices, insulation, identification and agreed testing.
No user soldering, crimping or cable splicing is included in the installation.
Quote harness work explicitly, even if performed by a subcontractor.
PCB finish: purple solder mask, white legend and ENIG.

CONNECTORS AND ORIENTATION
J1: eight male contacts underneath, 2.54 mm pitch.
J2: seven male contacts underneath. The VM position above J2 has no contact
and no plated hole. J1 and J2 row centers are 12.70 mm apart.
Long mating posts face DOWN into the controller. Do not reverse the headers.
J3: top-mounted keyed JST XH 4-position header, 2.50 mm pitch, cable entry UP.
The finished cable uses the matching XHP-4 housing and female crimp contacts.
J4: top-mounted two-position screw terminal, EXT+ and GND.
Its wire entry faces outward at the DIR end. SW1 sits toward the header rows,
so supply wires can leave the board without crossing the selector.
SW1: top-mounted non-shorting selector. Pad 2 is common, pad 1 is the protected
5 V buck output and pad 3 is the protected external supply. Verify actual
actuator direction against the final 5V/EXT labels on the first article.
J3, J4 and SW1 solder tails require controlled factory trimming where needed;
propose <=1.2 mm projection below the PCB without damaging the solder fillet.
Never trim the long J1/J2 mating posts. Confirm all projection and fit limits
on the assembly drawing before production.

POWER AND SELECTOR — EXTERNAL POWER IS REQUIRED IN BOTH MODES
Supply at J4         SW1 position       Common delivered to the driver
12 V or 24 V        5V                 Nominal 5 V, less diode/protection loss
Regulated 5 V       EXT                External 5 V, less protection loss
Regulated 24 V      EXT                External 24 V, less protection loss
12 V               EXT                Only for a driver explicitly allowing it

Use a regulated, appropriately protected DC source. Initial design scope is
nominal 5/12/24 V, with 4.75–26.4 V terminal range subject to qualification.
No 36/48 V input rating. Socket motor power is not used.
The switch changes the signal common only. Ground and logic power stay connected.
Switch only with controller and external supply power OFF and discharged.
Match the driver's own input-voltage selector/resistors to the delivered voltage.
For the provisional CL57T V4.1 profile, use its 5 V setting with adapter 5V mode
or external 5 V, and its 24 V setting with external 24 V. Its 12 V signal mode
is not established by this adapter's ability to pass 12 V.

SIGNAL CABLE — CAVITY NUMBERS CONTROL WIRING
J3.1 COM+  -> three separate finished leads: PUL+, DIR+, ENA+
J3.2 PUL-  -> PUL-
J3.3 DIR-  -> DIR-
J3.4 ENA-  -> ENA-
No motor, encoder, alarm or ground conductor occupies these four cavities.
Common-positive optocoupler input drivers are the intended topology.
This is a common-ground interface, not galvanically isolated or differential.

HARNESS FOR QUOTATION
500 +/-10 mm overall from plug mating face to each driver termination.
Last 80 mm forms the six-lead fanout. 24 AWG stranded copper, insulated for
at least 60 V DC, with finished outside diameter matching the JST contact.
JST XHP-4 housing; four SXH-001T-P0.6 contacts using qualified factory tooling.
Suggested colors: cavity 1 red, 2 white, 3 green, 4 black; numbering and durable
end labels take precedence over colors. The common conductor branches through
one fully insulated factory crimp splice or ultrasonic weld into three leads.
No solder-tinned crimp wires. No three wires packed into one driver ferrule.
One approved driver termination per lead. Exact ferrule size/length, terminal
strip length and torque remain subject to the actual driver terminal identity.
Factory to propose these details with its harness drawing before build release.
No bare-end substitution without a revised agreed cable specification.
Label both ends OEA-H1 r0.2. Identify the board end COM+/PUL-/DIR-/ENA-;
label the six driver ends PUL+, DIR+, ENA+, PUL-, DIR-, ENA- individually.
Test every intended connection, every possible unintended short, retention,
splice insulation, labels, strain relief and final length. Keep test records.

INSTALLATION AND FIRST ARTICLE
Identify the exact controller model/revision and drive model before energizing.
The electrical target is a standard StepStick socket with 3.3 V or 5 V STEP,
DIR and EN signals. Brand name alone does not establish the socket pinout.
The longer board overhang and populated underside require a non-powered fit
check with adjacent driver slots occupied and all intended cables connected.
Do not press the switch or tighten terminal screws against unsupported headers.
Support the adapter mechanically while handling connectors or terminal screws.
Mount the four-wire signal run away from motor and supply wiring; qualify the
actual cable length, routing and grounding with loaded timing measurements.

STARTUP AND ENABLE
A logic HIGH turns its output current sink ON. A logic LOW turns it OFF.
Host firmware must initialize STEP LOW before the buffer is enabled. A HIGH
STEP at release of the local power supervisor can create a pulse edge.
An unpowered adapter or open ENA input does not necessarily disable the driver.
This board is not an emergency stop, STO or independent machine inhibit.
The first article must establish the exact drive's EN polarity and startup,
reset, brownout, power-loss and reconnection behavior before machine use.

EVIDENCE
ERC, DRC, parts matching and document review are file checks. They do not prove
physical fit, loaded electrical operation, EMC, thermal behavior or a safe
machine state. The verification record must identify each check separately.
