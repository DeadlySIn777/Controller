OEA r0.3 cost and power architecture study — 2026-10-06
RESEARCH PROPOSAL ONLY. No r0.3 schematic, PCB, electrical release or order implied.
The partially routed r0.2 remains historical, unreleased work. It was not changed
by this study. Prices below are USD catalogue observations, not Amazon prices,
checkout totals, stocked supply commitments or a confirmed manufactured price.

RECOMMENDATION
Prototype three directly driven NX6008NBKR SOT-23 N-FET current sinks, a
conventional MP2459GJ-Z buck, external 5/12/24 V input, and a non-shorting 5V/EXT
selector. Remove the two LVC buffers, supervisor and 3.3 V LDO from the command
path. The separately requested voltage indicators still need their own design.
Use one SMT side, 0603 or larger passives, visible-lead SOT-23/TSOT23 packages,
ordinary plated vias and a two-layer 6/6 mil minimum design target. This should
be markedly easier to assemble on a controlled reflow hotplate than r0.2.
Actual fit, routing, paste stencil, solder profile and host clearance remain open.

DIRECT GATE DRIVE — SUPPORTING DATA AND LIMITS
Nexperia NX6008NBK data sheet specifies 60 V drain rating and +/-8 V gate rating.
At 25 C, maximum RDS(on) is 3 ohms at VGS=2.5 V/ID=200 mA, and 3.9 ohms at
1.8 V/50 mA. Maximum total gate charge is 0.7 nC at the specified 4.5 V gate,
30 V drain and 300 mA test; Ciss=27 pF and Crss=1.9 pF are typical, not maxima.
Pin mapping is 1 gate, 2 source, 3 drain. NX6008NBKR is the ordinary SOT-23 part,
not the smaller NBKW SOT-323 part used in r0.2. Generic 2N7002/BSS138 substitutions
are not automatically equivalent at 2.5 V gate drive.
https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/NX6008NBK.pdf

Proposed STEP/DIR: host pin -> 1 kohm -> gate; 10 kohm gate-to-source; source to
shared GND; drain to driver-minus. Host high asserts input current. COM+ is the
selected signal voltage and fans out to the external driver's three plus inputs.
This is a common-positive sink interface, not a differential transmitter.

Calculated screening, before tolerances: the 1 kohm resistor bounds the initial
current of a 5.5 V step to 5.5 mA, within the task's 6 mA host-drive screening
level. The gate divider produces 3.00 V from 3.3 V and 4.545 V from 5 V; static
host loads are 0.300/0.455 mA. To claim the 2.5 V on-resistance condition, require
actual guaranteed host VOH >=2.75 V before tolerances. At 20 mA, 3 ohms implies
60 mV sink drop and 1.2 mW dissipation at the stated data-sheet temperature.
Do not extrapolate that 25 C maximum into an unstated full-temperature guarantee.

The 6 mA figure is a VOH/VOL load specification, not proof of a particular
controller's maximum allowable capacitive load. Q/I=0.7 nC/6 mA=117 ns is only a
charge-time screening number; resistor current decreases during the transition.
Ciss alone misses Miller charge. Gate, drain and driver-terminal waveforms at
3.3/5 V, 5/12/24 V common and intended pulse widths must therefore be measured.
Do not publish a maximum STEP frequency from those arithmetic estimates.
Short local gate paths, a close pulldown and power-off cable connection are
important. Host low level, hot leakage, drain-edge coupling, host reset and
host-unpowered behavior remain qualification cases.

EN POLICY MUST BE EXPLICIT
The inexpensive baseline is the same series resistor/pulldown on EN. All sinks
are off with an undriven host; that does not mean the motor driver is disabled.
CL57T ENA-open behavior is specifically not a universal disable state. Firmware
must map EN polarity for the exact driver and initialize STEP low before common
power is applied. Host-high STEP at power application can create an input edge.
There is no remaining local power-good delay or brownout gating in this proposal.

An optional EN-only host-powered pullup may establish a reset default while VIO
exists. For example, 4.7 kohm from HOST_VIO to EN before the 1 kohm series resistor,
and 47 kohm gate pulldown, gives approximately 2.94 V at the gate from 3.3 V when
the host pin is high-impedance. It draws about 0.7 mA from a low-driving 3.3 V host.
That different EN network has a slower release and must be reviewed separately.
Never pull any host signal up to external 5/12/24 V. No local rail powers HOST_VIO.
Neither EN option provides STO, an emergency stop or host-loss motor inhibition.

BUCK CHOICE
AP63205WU-7 is attractive at $0.511 each/100 (Mouser observation). It uses a
visible-lead TSOT26, fixed 5 V output and a 3.8–32 V input range. It needs an
external inductor, bootstrap and input/output capacitors. However, the r0.2
SMAJ28CA clamp can reach 45.4 V at its stated pulse test. That does not protect a
32 V buck. A 24 V +/-10% steady source is not a sufficient transient analysis.
Do not transplant the old TVS and claim AP63205 is protected.
https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/AP63200-AP63201-AP63203-AP63205.pdf
https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Diodes-Incorporated/AP63205WU-7?qs=u16ybLDytRZtkj8PzdWCOw%3D%3D

Prefer MP2459GJ-Z for this cost study: manufacturer's direct page lists $0.86 at
100. It is active, TSOT23-6, and supports 4.5–55 V input. Its asynchronous circuit
adds a catch diode and feedback divider compared with the AP63205. Pins are
1 BST, 2 GND, 3 FB, 4 EN, 5 IN, 6 SW. The data sheet has 60 V absolute input max,
0.792–0.832 V feedback limits under the stated conditions, and 1.0–1.5 A switch
current limit. The high input rating is component margin, not a 48 V product
rating. Retain the product's 5/12/24 V regulated-source scope, max 26.4 V steady.
https://www.monolithicpower.com/en/products/power-management/switching-converters-controllers/step-down-buck/converters/mp2459.html
https://www.monolithicpower.com/media/document/MP2459_r1.01.pdf

Candidate support: 15 uH SRN5040-150M, 5x5x4 mm, 2 A listed saturation current,
0.096 ohm maximum DCR; $0.264 each/100. Its 4 mm height needs mechanical review.
The cheaper/smaller SRN4018-150M has only 1.1 A listed saturation current, below
the buck's 1.5 A maximum current limit, so it is not the fault-screened default.
Normal 60 mA load alone must not determine inductor saturation requirements.
Retain effective capacitance, inductor tolerance/hot saturation, startup and
short-circuit tests as selection gates. The MPS application circuit is a starting
point, not a finished BOM for this board.
https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/srn5040.pdf
https://www.mouser.com/ProductDetail/Bourns/SRN5040-150M?qs=U0ECReq1GB%252BK3VpL6%2Fxonw%3D%3D

MP2459 EN must not connect directly to 24 V. Its ordinary control pins have a
6 V absolute maximum. Review a divider and clamp for steady supply and the TVS
pulse envelope; the nominal 1.35 V EN threshold is not a guaranteed UVLO window.
Use an input-isolation diode for buck-only reverse flow and retain the selector
leg diode. Size the catch diode for switch peak current, not only average 60 mA;
the exact diode, capacitors and divider values are still unselected here.

POWER ENTRY AND SELECTOR
Retain a fuse, bidirectional input TVS, reverse-polarity P-FET, gate resistor and
gate-source zener. PMV100EPAR is an existing 60 V SOT-23 candidate, with a $0.411
each/100 catalogue observation; its low gate-voltage and hot resistance require
review for the direct-5 V path. A cheap high-resistance BSS84 replacement could
lose appreciable voltage at 60 mA and is not accepted just because VDS is high.
SMAJ28CA has 28 V stand-off and 45.4 V maximum clamp at the stated 8.8 A test.
That offers useful MP2459 input margin, but PCB inductance, fuse I-squared-t,
TVS pulse energy and available source current still require coordination.
https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/PMV100EPA.pdf
https://www.mouser.com/es/ProductDetail/Nexperia/PMV100EPAR?qs=vmHwEFxEFR%2FYGVY%2F8WC8mQ%3D%3D
https://www.littelfuse.com/~/media/electronics/datasheets/tvs_diodes/littelfuse_tvs_diode_smaj_datasheet.pdf.pdf

Use external 5 V only in EXT position. Use 12/24 V with 5V or EXT as the driver
profile requires. Ground remains continuous. No VM socket connection is needed.
Retain E-Switch EG1218 non-shorting SPDT as the priced selector candidate, 0.2 A
at 30 VDC, $0.494 each/100. Retain a reverse-blocking diode on its regulated-5 V
leg and a small common discharge resistor. Switch only with power off and
discharged: break-before-make does not remove stored charge from a connected
cable/driver input. Verify actual actuator direction against finished labels.
https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/E-Switch/EG1218?qs=xDsBkp9LkocT0c8K%252B5e%2FgA%3D%3D
https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/e-switch/EG1218/101726

The selector diode means a nominal 5 V buck does not deliver exactly 5 V at the
driver. Do not silently raise the regulator setpoint or sense after the diode
without reviewing accuracy, no-load behavior and loop stability. Delivered
voltage/current must meet the target driver's minimum and maximum. The supply
can pass 12 V, but that alone does not qualify a particular driver's 12 V input.

that architecture for the user's requested combined adapter.
