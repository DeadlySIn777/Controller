OEA r0.3 independent circuit proposal review — 2026-10-06

Scope: independent primary-source pin/polarity review and arithmetic screening.
This is not SPICE, native ERC/DRC, physical validation or fabrication release.
The accompanying receipt identifies the exact source snapshots. The controller
motherboard and previous adapter revisions were not edited by this review.

Interface and indicator

The three proposed direct sinks retain the A4988-style STEP/DIR/EN positions,
continuous shared ground and physical omission of VM. HOST_VIO is not connected.
NX6008NBKR pin1=gate,2=source,3=drain matches the standard SOT-23 assignment.
J3 remains common-positive, pulse-minus, direction-minus, enable-minus. A high
host signal turns its sink on. A high-impedance host releases the sink after
gate discharge; this does not guarantee that the external driver is disabled.

The ATtiny202 SOIC pin assignments and AP7375-SA / MP2459 pin assignments are
consistent with their manufacturer drawings. Indicator GPIOs do not share
motion, ADC or UPDI signals. The LED cathodes are grounded and each anode has
its own 1k series resistor. Keep all LEDs off until settled, calibrated reads.
The <=5MHz CPU choice is consistent with the possible low end of the selected
brownout threshold; a nominal 2.6V BOD would not prove the >=2.7V needed at10MHz.

The 100k/3.9k,0.1% ADC divider reaches 0.992862V at26.4V in its maximum-ratio
resistor corner. This leaves approximately52mV beneath the 1.045V reference
screen. The reference's characterization tolerance is not complete ADC system
accuracy. Two-point calibration and measured LED boundaries remain necessary.
The source resistance is3.754k and100nF gives approximately0.375ms nominal RC.
The conservative startup injection limit is26.4/99.9k=0.2643mA, below the MCU's
stated15mA conditional absolute injection limit. This permits bounded injection;
it does not promise accurate ADC results or functional operation during it.
No programmer power may be applied to V_IND_3V3; power through the normal input.

Regulated5V mode and CL57T

Using124k/23.7k feedback resistors at0.1%, the MP2459 reference-and-resistor
screen is4.92752–5.19380V before D25. The diode's0.4V maximum at100mA/25C gives
a4.52752V lower COM screen. Its datasheet specifies no minimum forward drop,
so an exact5.0V upper limit at the driver is not established by these data.
Feedback bias, temperature, ripple and cable drop remain outside that screen.
At20mA, the direct FET's3.9ohm/1.8V-gate/25C maximum contributes78mV drop.
The proposed4.5–5.5V LED class covers the arithmetic COM screen, but has only
27.5mV lower-bound margin before measurement error/ripple. Measure the loaded
boundary; revise calibrated UI guard bands if needed without calling a weak
signal healthy. The LED denotes nominal voltage class, not driver approval.

The CL57T V4.1 manual specifies3.5–5V pulse/direction high levels in5V mode,
and7–16mA logic current. Its5V figure is not labelled an absolute damage limit;
slightly above5V at no load is neither proof of damage nor a guaranteed accepted
corner. Verify each active input at the driver through the actual cable, with
one and all three channels loaded. Direct12V use is a different CL57T profile:
its manual requires5V selector mode plus series resistance for PUL/DIR. ENA is
separately5–24V compatible. Never put one shared current-setting resistor in
COM. Initial generic12V support must not imply direct12V CL57T compatibility.

Power-down and inrush corrections

The AP7375 has a VOUT−VIN absolute limit of0.3V and no reverse-protection claim.
The isolated input reservoir plus output bleed is a coherent normal-collapse
mitigation. With Cin=3.51uF,Cout=2.9095uF and4.7k+1% bleed, the minimum output
bleed at a0.3V boundary is63.2uA. This permits about76.2uA of input-only drain
in the simplified boundary calculation, versus12.5uA used in the proposal.
However, its5uA input-current maximum is specified at12V/25C, not throughout
undervoltage. Therefore the arithmetic is a prototype screen, not a guaranteed
shutdown proof. Scope VOUT−VIN through input removal/short and repeated ramps.
Input and output capacitors must retain their controlled values; do not add
output bulk or externally driven programming power. Scope the ADC node too.

The original68ohm indicator feed allowed a conservative392mA normal26.4V
startup bound.82ohm reduces that to325mA, below BAT46W's350mA repetitive pulse
rating under its stated pulse conditions. With0.1% feedback and5mA load, the
25C low-input screen is4.52752−0.45−(82.82*0.005)=3.66342V at the LDO input.
Transient energy, cold forward drop and actual cap charging remain bench cases.

An unselected buck originally had only its feedback divider as output load.
Its input quiescent draw and much larger output reservoir could make VIN fall
below the charged output; D22 alone does not prove SW<=VIN+0.3V. A330ohm0805
V5_REG bleeder corrects that decay mismatch. At2.65V and333.3ohm it discharges
7.9508mA. Cin3.4uF/Cout27.83uF then permits about971uA input drain, versus an
approximately927uA screen including input IQ, EN divider and diode leakage.
The330ohm resistor dissipates82.6mW at the high output/resistance corner, within
its125mW rating for the proposed0–50C ambient. Below-UVLO current is not fully
specified, so test this exact unselected-buck shutdown case. A90mA design load
allows the driver, indicator and added bleed under the stated normal budgets.

Result

The corrected proposal is suitable to continue into prototype CAD. There is
no manufactured-board acceptance implied. Current waveform, startup/shutdown,
regulation and actual host fit must be checked on the prototype. This review
does not require complete product environmental certification before CAD.
Keep costs provisional: the50x15.24mm two-layer calculator dimensions are a
packing target; the $15 retail target includes more than bare PCB and parts.
