XHP-4 is the intended XH 2.50 mm mating housing for the r0.3 B4B-XH-A header. Do not replace with unspecified 2.54 mm connectors.
Wire colors from Cosel are black, black, red, red at positions 1, 2, 3, 4. Label by actual connector pin number and continuity, not color: r0.3 pin 1 COM+, pin 2 PUL-, pin 3 DIR-, pin 4 ENA-. In particular the first black wire is COM+, not ground. None of these four wires is adapter ground. The driver-end common needs an appropriate bridge to its three positive control terminals according to the qualified driver profile.

UPDI PROGRAMMER
Recommended exact product: Adafruit 5879 UPDI Friend (standard 3 V/5 V version, not high-voltage version).
Quantity: 1 shared tool for all adapters.
Preferred consolidated source: DigiKey 1528-5879-ND.
https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/adafruit-industries-llc/5879/22596413
Observed: 112 in stock, USD 6.95 at quantity 1.
Mouser alternative: 485-5879; source https://www.mouser.com/es/ProductDetail/Adafruit/5879?qs=ZcfC38r4PovNAVhT%252B2hQ2A%3D%3D showed 22 stock and USD 6.95.
Manufacturer product: https://www.adafruit.com/product/5879
Pinout/operation reference: https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-updi-friend/pinouts
The included JST-SH lead ends in individual male pins. Select the programmer's 3 V setting. For this adapter connect white UPDI to TP50 and black GND to TP52; leave its red PWR wire disconnected/insulated. TP51 is voltage-sense-only on the adapter and must not receive power from this programmer. Power the adapter through J4 during programming.
The current PCB has bare test pads, so the included male-pin lead does not plug straight into the adapter. Temporary soldered leads or a properly designed pogo fixture are still required; the programmer is not evidence that programming setup/firmware is complete. A USB-C data cable to the computer is required and is not listed as included by Adafruit.

REJECTED AS EXACT 500 MM FOUR-WIRE REPLACEMENTS
Adafruit 5497 is 500 mm but only 2 wires.
Adafruit 4874 is four-position XH-compatible but only about 200 mm per pigtail.
Neither meets the requested finished 500 mm four-wire harness scope.

PURCHASE/ENGINEERING BOUNDARY
These parts can be staged in carts, but the r0.3 PCB remains an untested prototype candidate; final routing, physical host fit, voltage-indicator firmware, calibrated thresholds and prototype qualification are not complete. This sourcing report does not release a board for manufacture or establish a $15 retail price.
