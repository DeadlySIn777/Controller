Price decisions
- Headers: replaced B0FRZW75VS (USD 6.29) with B0HG4YZ3BG (USD 4.79), both
  free delivery and the same published pitch/length/material class.
- 5.1 V zener: replaced B099687PB8, 100 pieces / USD 11 free delivery, with
  B0HFGM5Q8G, 20 pieces / USD 7.13 plus USD 1 delivery. Saves USD 2.87 before
  tax. The -13-F packaging-only equivalence is supported by the manufacturer:
  https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/BZT52Cxx.pdf
- Programmer: selected B0H2LMD4J5, two USB Serial UPDI boards / USD 20 plus
  USD 5.70 delivery, instead of the reviewed generic 5879 listing at USD
  31.16 delivered. Product photographs show 3 V / 5 V selection and UPDI,
  PWR and GND connections. This is a generic compatible prototype candidate,
  not authenticated Adafruit hardware. Bench verification remains open.
- MP2459GJ-Z: retained 10-piece B0BDSW5RH3 at USD 6.92 + USD 5.50 shipping;
  reviewed exact alternatives cost USD 11.39 + 2.39 or USD 14 + 3.99.
- ATtiny202: retained three 2-piece packs at USD 6.43 each. The reviewed
  one-piece offers cost more for the required five devices.
- BAT46W-7-F: retained USD 4.99 exact pack. Another exact listing was USD
  6.44 + 2 delivery. Combined AVLIS shipping makes the retained pack cheaper.
- BZT52C12-7-F and SMAJ28CA-13-F: retained USD 2.99 exact packs; reviewed
  alternatives did not reduce the combined cost with equivalent evidence.
- Chanzon 0603 assortment could reduce common resistor purchase cost, but
  lacked a numeric working-voltage/TCR specification and manufacturer
  datasheet; not silently substituted. Precision, pulse, capacitance,
  dielectric, voltage and footprint requirements were retained.
